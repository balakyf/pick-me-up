# Lane I · Boss presentation and battle bodies — notes

> **Status:** done · **Date:** 2026-10-02 · **Base:** `f282864` · **Branch:** this worktree's branch
> **Brief:** pillars 3 and 4; O8 and O15 in the research synthesis: anchor floors look like set pieces, and bodies move.
> Nothing in `src/engine` changed: no combat value, roll, golden test or sim number moved (presentation only), so no sim run was needed.

## What shipped, brief item by item

### 1. Boss intro (`battle/bossIntro.ts`, `BossIntro.tsx`, `useBossShow.tsx`)

- **`BOSS_INTRO`** (keyed by template id): name, epithet, colour and tier for every anchor boss and raid boss (Black Priest, the Lv999 Creature, Halgiraf, the Lizardman Chief, Kurushahr, the Stone Statue, Kthat, Valention, Versace, Darkan, the Egg, the Inquisitor, El Cid, the Saint, the Chimera Matriarch, Pryos, the Fragment Colossus, the Herald, Tell), the lieutenants (Rodvick, Lazenca, the Jewel Guardian, the Kraken, the Mimic) and Tell's five echoes. A test holds it to the content: every anchor's mission target and every raid boss (`CHALLENGE.raids.bosses`) has a card, every echo template is an echo.
- **When:** `bossShows(log)` finds the beat a carded foe first steps onto the field — battle start, a wave, or a summon — once per template per fight (Valention's recalled Rodvick is not introduced twice). Several arrivals share one card (main first: boss over lieutenant over echo, mission target first): "THE BLACK PRIEST · SHEPHERD OF THE FALLING CITY — with Lv999 Creature · Do Not Wake It".
- **The card:** kicker (Boss / Lieutenant / Echo · Wave 2/2 — the wave banner stands aside), `NAME · EPITHET`, level and "Phase 1 of N" from lane G's phase keywords. The stage dims around the arrival (a radial spotlight inside the camera), the camera eases in toward it (WAAPI on `.battle-cam`), the card slams down (steps), then everything eases out.
- **Timing:** the beat holds `INTRO_MS` 2000 (1300 for a lieutenant or echo) longer at 1×, divided by the speed (a glance at 4×). `frameHold` takes a `shows` map (`showHoldsFor`), `replayLength(log, { shows: true })` counts it.
- **Skippable:** a click anywhere on the card (or its Skip button), or **Esc**, moves on to the next beat.
- **Reduced motion:** no camera ease, the card and the dim only fade.
- **French:** `slices/frBossShow.ts` (every name, epithet and UI string; the echoes' names and drafts stay lane G's).

### 2. Boss bar (`battle/bossBar.ts` pure, `BossBar.tsx`)

- Sits under the caption in the top bar's middle column (`stage-top-mid`), so lane E's mission HUD (left) and turn strip (right) keep their places; on a phone (narrow/beside) it docks full width under the caption with the epithet and % dropped.
- **Follows** the living tier-boss the mission names, else the latest to arrive; once all have fallen it stays, empty. (F10: the Black Priest, not the creature.)
- **HP** with lane E's ghost drain (`.hpbar > .hp-ghost`), the % on the bar.
- **Phase notches** at lane G's `atHpPct` thresholds, lit once `BossView.passed` counts them; titles in the tooltip.
- **Aegis pips:** charges the template (and the anchor's group) gives, plus those its passed phases add, less every `guard` beat on it (Pryos 3 → 1 → 3 at the second seal…).
- **Clock:** the soonest enrage still coming among the bosses on the field ("Lv999 Creature enrages" at F10, Halgiraf's own at F20), else the mission's deadline (not a survival: lane E's HUD has that bell). Once enraged — by its tick or by a phase like El Cid's ×1.3 — the bar turns red: "ENRAGED ×1.3".
- The guild's stand-in Colossus (a renamed unit with no keywords) shows its own name and no template keywords.

### 3. Big boss sprites (`pixel/bossSprite.ts`, `pixel/shape.ts`)

- **Native resolution**, deterministic, facing the party: El Cid (56×68), Versace (54×64), Valention (62×72), Pryos (60×72), the Herald (70×86), Tell (58×80), Darkan (66×72), the Chimera Matriarch (92×66); and a size between hero and boss for the Black Priest (40×52), Rodvick (44×50) and Lazenca (40×48). Halgiraf and Kthat keep their native 76×62 dragons (now with a wingbeat). `bossScale` (the 1.6× nearest-neighbour upscale) is gone.
- **`shape.ts`:** `poly` (scanline fill), `thick` strokes, `disc`, `arc`, and `paint`, which fills a shape and shades it from its own silhouette (lit edge, rim shadow, underside) so each part reads as a solid form; `idleFrame` (a generic breath or bob). Flecks (Versace's lightning, the Herald's hem) come from `rand.ts`.
- **Echoes still work** and got better: `echoOf` now stretches the boss's own light range over the spectral ramp, so the silver El Cid keeps its detail (a fixed curve washed it to a silhouette) and the dark Halgiraf stays light enough.
- **Tests (`pixel.test.ts`):** every template has two idle frames of one size, both drawn, different; the bosses are ≥ 46 px tall (the six set pieces ≥ 64) and not 2×2-blocky (no upscales); boss drawers are pure; echoes keep their boss's size and shape. **DevGallery** has a row of every boss sprite (both frames) and a row of every class's poses.

### 4. Poses (`pixel/heroSprite.ts`, `pixel/sprites.ts`, `battle/battlePose.ts`, `UnitSprite.tsx`)

- **`drawHeroPose(look, pose)`** for `idle` (the other half of the breath), `attack` (a stride, the body leaning in, the weapon out front), `cast` (the weapon or staff raised before the face, motes of the element), `hurt` (rocked back, eyes screwed shut, arm flung back), `guard` (a crouch, the weapon or the shield brought to the front), `victory` (weapon to the sky) and `ko` (the side view laid on its back on the floor of a 32×32 box, eyes closed). It reuses `drawSide` with posed arms (`poseArm` / `weaponPose`) per weapon.
- **Enemies:** `drawEnemy(id, el, frame)`: the bosses and dragons author their second frame (breath, cape and hair sway, wingbeat, the Herald's eyes blinking, Pryos's seal turning, the creature's eyes closing); everything else gets `idleFrame` (a breath; floaters bob).
- **URLs:** `heroPoseUrl`, `allyPoseUrl` (the Sealed Object keeps its one frame), `enemyUrl(name, el, frame)`, `poseSize`. Keys are bounded (seven poses, two frames): no animation state in a key.
- **Choice per frame (`heroPoseFor`, tested):** dead → `ko` (during the death moment `hurt`, with the fall animation now turning the body the way the KO pose lies); a won fight → `victory` (with lane E's cheer jump); acting → `cast` for magic, support skills (no blow of their own) and anything a mage does, `guard` for a brace, else `attack`; struck → `hurt`; braced (a `guard-up` status, or a blow turned on it) → `guard`; otherwise the two idle frames.
- **UnitSprite** stacks the two idle frames and alternates them with a CSS step animation (paused by lane E's hit-stop, still under reduced motion); a fallen posed hero lies in its KO bitmap (no CSS rotation).

### 5. Skill VFX (`battle/skillFx.ts`, `BattleFxCanvas.tsx`)

- **`SKILL_FX`**: hand-picked profiles for every active hero skill (30) and 66 of lane G's enemy skills; **`defaultFx`** gives anything else (lane J's new skills too) a profile from its element, targets and effects (heal → motes, shield/taunt → dome, summon → portal, buff/debuff → rising/sinking chevrons, poison → cloud, burn → flames, stun → stars, multi-hit → flurry, magic sweep → rune or void, light → pillar, cleave → thrust, physical sweep → crescent sweep). A test checks every castable skill has a profile.
- **24 shapes**: rune circle (Arcane Burst), slash, sweep (a crescent running down the line: Incident), thrust, flurry, thunder columns (Thunder Volley, Thunderclap), heal motes, shield dome, poison cloud, flames, breath cone (Halgiraf's Dragon Breath, Inferno), light pillar, blade rain (Sword Rain, Iron Verdict), quake (Titan Fist, Colossal Slam), wave (Tidal Wave), void rings (Dark Dominion, End of Days), aura up/down, stars, chains, bite, impact ring, portal (Hatch, Call the Void), pages (Tell's Rewrite, Final Draft).
- **`fxOps(cast, t)`** is pure: an effect at an instant as pixel rectangles, deterministic from its seed (no `Math.random`), tested for every shape (draws mid-flight, fades by its end, same output twice). `castTargets` finds everyone a cast reaches.
- **Canvas:** at most 6 effects at once, frozen by hit-stop, drawn in whole logical pixels over the sparks. A peak flash only when the flashes setting is on and motion is not reduced. A phone keeps at most 220 particles (500 on desktop). A melee striker's effect waits until they arrive.

### 6. Finisher

- The beat with a tier-boss's death (`bossShows` finisher; the Lv999 Creature counts when someone does kill it) plays in **slow motion** (the stage's `--spd`/`--pop-spd`, the impact juice and the popups run at 0.4×; the beat holds `FINISHER_MS` 1500 longer at 1×), a **white-out** as the killing blow lands (skipped when flashes are off; soft under reduced motion), a heavy shake (the screen-shake setting), three bursts in the boss's element, and the boss's own sprite **shattering** into 40 seeded triangles that fly apart (`shatter.ts`, `BossShatter.tsx`; under reduced motion they fade in place).
- **The Lv999 Creature's `wakes` beat** gets its moment: a red title card ("IT WAKES"), a red dim around it, the camera easing in and two heavy shakes (`WAKES_MS` 1500).

### 7. Layout for big bodies (found in the browser)

Twice-a-hero bosses in a 66-px vertical band overlapped their adds (F10's creature and priest, Versace and his soldiers, Tell and his echoes). `layout(log, squeeze, sizeOf)`: a wave holding a foe ≥ 48 px tall forms one rank — the towering ones abreast from the front line (alternating a little up and down), the rest in one or two files behind them, closing ranks to fit the stage. Waves without one keep lane E's layout exactly.

## Decisions

- **The bar lives under the caption,** not in its own row: lane E's top bar keeps its grid, the bar inherits its phone docking, and the caption above it still reads.
- **Cards once per template per fight,** and arrivals together share one card: a recalled lieutenant or a second echo of a kind does not stop the fight again.
- **The wave banner stands aside** for a boss's card (the card names the wave).
- **The finisher fires on any tier-boss death,** including the Lv999 Creature if a party kills it: slaying the unkillable deserves it.
- **Poses are fixed bitmaps** keyed by look and pose (seven), the idle by two stacked images and CSS; nothing time-dependent reaches `render.ts`'s caches.
- **Skill VFX are canvas rectangles from a pure function** rather than sprite sheets: deterministic, testable, cheap, and they scale with the stage like lane E's sparks.
- **`replayLength` counts the shows only when asked** (`{ shows: true }`): the sim's `fightStats` keeps the numbers it had.

## Left for later

- Kurushahr, the Inquisitor, the Saint, the Lizardman Chief, the Kraken, the Stone Statue and the Fragment Colossus have cards and bars but still their earlier sprites (hero-sized humanoids or the golem/kraken scales); a native pass for them is the natural next art job.
- Humanoid foes built on hero looks could use `drawHeroPose` (mirrored) for an attack pose; today foes only breathe and lunge.
- `src/sim/fightStats.replayMs` could call `replayLength(log, { cutIns: true, shows: true })` to time exactly what the scene plays (anchors gain ~2–5 s at 1×).
- The Herald and Tell were checked only with boosted heroes (no bot reaches them).

## Review fixes (adversarial review)

- `battle.css`: a stray `}` after `@keyframes heroFall` swallowed the next rule, so `.death-vignette` (the defeat banner's vignette) never applied.
- A shattered boss stayed broken only during its finisher beat: on the next beat its `shattered` class went away and the KO dissolve replayed, so the boss popped back at full opacity and faded a second time. `useBossShow` now returns `gone` (finishers already played) and those bosses keep the class.
- The boss bar's aegis pips walk the event stream (phases add as they turn, guards spend) and a blow that lands proves the aegis empty: F90's subverted Herald (stripped of his aegis by the truths) no longer shows three pips he does not have.
- A carded boss steps in without lane E's entry stagger, so at 4× the spotlight no longer sits on empty ground while it waits its turn to march in.
- Skipping a card (click or Esc) cancels the camera's ease-in, so the next beats do not play zoomed in.
