# Lane M · Story: notes

> **Status:** done · **Date:** 2026-10-04 · **Base:** `530a3f2` (waves 1–6 merged) · **Branch:** `worktree-agent-afa8612f6909d7ae3`
> **Brief:** pillar 4, "every anchor is an event": anchor briefings, boss lines, act cards, the Priasis arc, Isel's eulogy, and the F90 Herald's story treatment. Lane N (hero sheet, picker, gear) ran in parallel.

## What shipped

### 1 · The story data (`src/engine/content/story.ts`)

Pure data, no imports, no state. The UI translates every line with `t()`.

- **`ACT_STORY`** (one per `ACTS` id): the act's name, a one-line epigraph, and Isel's word.
- **`ANCHOR_STORY`** (one per `ANCHORS` floor, all 22): a title, *who* is up there, *why* it matters, Isel's word, an optional **opening** line (an escort or Isel speaks on the fight's first action: F5, F15, F25, F45, F50), and the **aftermath** the results show after a clear.
- **`BOSS_LINES`** (keyed by template id, 25 bosses, lieutenants and beasts): `voice` (`speaks` or `narrated`: a beast or a thing is narrated in italics), `entrance`, `telegraph` keyed by the **charged skill id** (Halgiraf says one thing as he draws breath and another as he folds his wings to dive), `phases` keyed by phase number, `defeat`, and `victory` (over a party that lost).
  - **Phase lines only where lane G's phase line is narration** (Halgiraf "beats his black wings…", Kthat "eyes go white…"). Where lane G already gave the boss spoken words (Valention, El Cid, Pryos, the Herald, Tell, Versace), the phase card already carries them, and a second quote would only clutter.
- **`PRIASIS_ARC`**: 12 beats unlocked by `highestCleared` (and, for the last two, by the world's fate). Each beat may carry a letter (from Priasis, or from Isel once Priasis can no longer write) and Isel's lobby line.
- **`HERALD`**: the F90 framing: the Herald's own word, what Clear means, what Subvert means, what the unknowing Master is left with, and the two aftermaths.

### 2 · Anchor briefings (war room)

`ui/story/AnchorBriefing.tsx`, mounted as one line among `TowerScreen`'s command-panel banners (after the loop banner, hidden while an event floor waits). It shows a kicker (Briefing · Floor n · mission), the title, the floor's **face** (the mission's own boss, else the person to protect: Priasis at F15, the Key Bearer at F45), who/why, and Isel's word with her bust. At F90 (before the world's fate is sealed) it adds the Herald's quote and the two ways through (Subvert is greyed when the Master cannot reach the truths). Lane C's TowerScreen is not restructured.

### 3 · Boss lines in battle

`ui/story/storyBeats.ts` (pure) + `ui/story/StoryBox.tsx` (`useStoryBox`), mounted with **one hook call and one node** in `BattleScene` (beside `boss.card`).

- **Beats it rides** (no new `CombatEvent` kinds): lane I's `bossShows` (intro → entrance, finisher → defeat), lane G's `phase` and `telegraph` events, the first `act` (an anchor's opening), and the end of the replay (victory over a lost party, the floor's boss still standing).
- **The entrance comes on the beat after the title card**: the card names the boss, then the boss speaks, and on a phone the box never covers the card.
- **Wind-ups:** the first two wind-ups of each charged move speak; after that the ring and caption still warn, in silence. A wind-up that gives way to another line on its beat is not counted.
- **Durations (lane rule 8):** `STORY_MS` per kind (entrance 3000, phase 2800, telegraph 1800, defeat 2400, opening 2600 ms at 1×, divided by the replay speed). The box starts on its beat, lingers for its time even if the replay moves on, and gives way to a newer line. The victory line stays until the Master leaves.
- **Layout:** a JRPG text box (face, name in the boss's title-card colour, the line), screen space, `pointer-events: none`. A foe's line sits under the foes (left), an ally's under the party (right); on a phone it spans the stage. Reduced motion: a plain fade.
- Only the anchor's own fight carries the opening line (`log.mission.type` must match the anchor's mission), so a raid or a trial on the same floor number does not.

### 4 · Act cards

`ui/story/ActCard.tsx`. The first time the Master stands in a new act: "ACT II · FLOORS 11–20", the act's name, the epigraph and Isel's word over the act's own backdrop (`drawBattleBg(act.from)`, cached per theme), with a slow pan and a timer line.

- **A chapter title, not a question:** it lifts on its own after `ACT_CARD_MS` (6.5 s), a click or Esc lifts it sooner, and it is **not** `.overlay`/`[role=dialog]`, so the hotkeys keep working (the QoL smoke test travels T → U through it). Reduced motion: no slam, no pan.
- **Once per save:** latched as it shows (TowerScreen keeps it on screen locally) through the existing `GUIDE_STEP` command under `story:act:<id>`. `storySeen(state, key)` is the defaulting reader (an old save, or one without the key, has not seen it). **No new command and no schema bump** (decision below).
- It waits while a battle, the results, the Enter sheet, the ballista, an event outcome or a replay holds the screen, so clearing F10 shows the results first and Act II's card after.

### 6 · Isel's eulogy

`ui/story/eulogy.ts` replaces lane K's `ISEL_FOR` pool. `memorialBand.iselFor` keeps its signature and delegates; the identity comes off the grave (`FallenRecord` has id, name, star, class and portrait token), so nothing new is stored. Three short sentences:

1. **who they were**: the hero's voice (`personalityOf`: formal, rough, cheerful, quiet, grim; two lines each) and the trade before the crystal (`tradeName`);
2. **what they were like**: their trait's family (`traitOf`, ten lines);
3. **what is left**: the mourners by name (5 lines), else a newcomer's welcome (under a day), else the anchor they fell at, named by its story ("They fell at The Half Black Dragon, floor 20…"), else the floor they reached.

Deterministic (every pick hashes the hero id); with lane K's `taken` set, one battle's dead never share a sentence while a pool lasts. A grave without an identity (an older save) gets "Name. <closing line>". Lane K's closing lines (`ISEL_CLOSE`) stay.

### 7 · The F90 Herald

The briefing frames the choice (above). The Enter sheet's world section opens with the Herald's word (`HeraldWord`, one line in `EnterConfirm`). The Herald speaks in battle (entrance, wind-up of *End of Days*, defeat: "You won. Remember that you chose to.", victory). The results banner carries `HERALD.saved` or `HERALD.ended` as the aftermath. **The mechanics are unchanged**: Clear ends the world; Subvert with seven truths strips the aegis.

## Decisions

- **The "seen" latch uses the existing guide list** (`life.guide.done`, through `GUIDE_STEP`, under a `story:` prefix) instead of a new optional field and a new command. It persists like every other guide step, needs no `Command` union append (lane N may append to the same union at the same time), and `storySeen` defaults to "not seen" for any save. `FirstSteps` only counts its own ids, so the extra keys are inert there.
- **Telegraph lines are keyed by the charged skill**, not a rotating pool, so a line never contradicts the move (the first browser pass showed "Breathe in…" over a Sky Dive). The tests require every charged move of a speaking boss to have a line, and every line to name a charged move that boss can wind up.
- **No engine change.** `content/story.ts` is data the engine never imports; combat, the golden test and the determinism guard are untouched, so the sim was not re-run (the last numbers stand: casual 59/79/79, engaged 84/89/79, whale 89/89/84).
- **The briefing sits above the forecast.** The story card comes first, then the odds. On a phone it adds about 200 px to the command column (the sticky Enter panel still floats over the column, as before).
- Bosses with a spoken lane G phase line get no story phase line (see §1).
- **Tell is "she"** (canon: the goddess). The Herald gets no pronoun ("its aegis"): lanes C and I disagree, and the lines do not need one.

## What is left

### Hooks for lane O (endgame, epilogue, credits, New Cycle)

- **The choice's words** are `HERALD` in `content/story.ts` (`herald`, `clear`, `subvert`, `unknowing`, `ended`, `saved`). The briefing (`AnchorBriefing`) and the Enter sheet (`HeraldWord`) read them; the F90 mechanics are unchanged (`tower.ts` §6d, `TUNING.lifecycle.subvertTruths`).
- **The aftermath** is `aftermathLine(result)` in `ui/story/StoryNotes.tsx`: `worldSaved` → `HERALD.saved`, `worldEnded` → `HERALD.ended`. An epilogue screen can open from the same result.
- **The Priasis arc ends on the world's fate:** beats with `when: 'ended' | 'saved'` (after 90). Lane O can append epilogue beats (an F100 beat, a New Cycle beat) to `PRIASIS_ARC`; `priasisBeats` filters them by `when` already. A new `when` value (say `'cycle'`) needs one line in `priasisBeats`.
- **The credits' fallen:** `eulogy(input)` (or `iselFor(name, grave, mourners)`) gives Isel's words for any grave in `state.life.memorial`, deterministic per hero. A credits roll can call it per grave.
- **Once-per-save presentation:** `storySeen(state, key)` and `storyStep(key)` (the `story:` guide latch). An epilogue that should play once can latch `story:epilogue:<fate>` the same way.
- **After the world ends**, `ACT_STORY.void` (Act VIII's card) still reads as before F90 ("no one finished building the tower"), which is true but not mournful. A post-end variant belongs with lane O's dead-world presentation. `ANCHOR_STORY[95]` and `[100]` are written for either fate.
- **Tell's victory line** ("Back to the first floor. I will write you a new world.") foreshadows the New Cycle; Tell's echoes (`echo_*`) have no lines of their own (the intro card names them). Lane O can add `BOSS_LINES.echo_*` entries; the tests already require each to be a real template with a title card.

### Other

- **Lane P (missions):** filler floors have no story; a filler floor could borrow its act's epigraph or a seeded line. Lane P's teaching curve could use the act card as its chapter break.
- **Lane H (audio):** the text box plays no sound; a soft "blip" per line (or a voice timbre per speaker) is a one-line cue in `useStoryBox`.
- **Lane L (life):** heroes could react to the arc in the lobby (a hero who escorted Priasis mourns her after F40). `latestPriasis(state)` is the query.
- **The F45 key** is left open on purpose ("it was hers"); a later lane could pay it off at F90 or F100.
- The briefing face for huge bosses (the Herald, Halgiraf) is the whole sprite shrunk to 40 px; a bespoke bust per boss would read better.
