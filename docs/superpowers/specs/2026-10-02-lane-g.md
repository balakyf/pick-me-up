Bosses now fight like themselves. Halgiraf draws a deep breath, and at half his HP he takes to
the air, where blades barely reach him and he dives on the hero he picks. El Cid stops holding
back. Valention calls Rodvick and Lazenca back to his side. Pryos closes a new seal over himself
at each third of his HP. The Egg hatches its brood, the Herald calls the void, and Tell's three
drafts bring back echoes of the anchors the Master already beat. The big moves are telegraphed
and can be answered: a stun or a kill cancels them, Guard and Protect soften them, and a retreat
escapes them. The Master has four new orders, a free pre-battle mark, and free Protects.

### 2. Telegraphs

- **`SkillDef.charge`** = `{ turns, line }`. The caster spends its turn winding up: SP and the
  cooldown are paid, and a **`telegraph`** event is emitted
  `{ unitId, skillId, firesAtTick, targets }`. `firesAtTick` is now plus one of the caster's
  turns at its current speed. Its gauge then stands still: `ready` and the fill skip it.
- **The move fires on that tick.** This happens at the start of the tick, after the Master's
  orders and before the statuses or anyone else act. It is an `act` with `charged: true`, which
  is not a turn of its own. A single blow still aims where it was telegraphed if it can. A sweep
  strikes whoever stands then.
- **Answers:**
  - A **stun** cancels it: a `telegraph-end` event with reason `stunned` ("the Dragon Breath dies
    in its throat").
  - The caster's **death** cancels it: `telegraph-end` with reason `fell`.
  - **Guard** braces the party, which takes 30 % less (a `guard-up` status on everyone on the
    party's side). It holds until every move now wound up has fired, and at least one average
    party turn.
  - **Protect** halves a charged blow on a protected hero.
  - **Retreat** ends the fight before the move fires.
  - The charged `act` says how it was met (`answered: 'guard' | 'protect'`). The caption reads
    "Halgiraf unleashes Sky Dive — the party has braced for it!".
- **In the replay** (`Telegraph.tsx`, `bossBattle.css`):
  - a '!' inside a countdown ring that fills toward the fire, with the move's name, over the
    caster;
  - a red reticle under every threatened unit;
  - the wind-up line as the caption, for example "Halgiraf folds his wings for a dive at Lyra
    Delcut!";
  - a Guard button that glows while a move is winding up.

  Under reduced motion the mark does not pulse.

### 3. Phases

- **`KeywordTag` `phase`:** `{ atHpPct, title, line, addKeywords, skills, spdPct, summonWave,
  cleanse }`.
  - The first blow that brings the boss to `atHpPct` triggers it, once, in order.
  - **A blow never carries a boss past a phase it has not reached.** The damage stops at the
    threshold, so every phase plays.
  - The new keywords and skills ride on a fresh `ref`. An `aegis` keyword adds charges.
  - `spdPct` changes the boss's speed for good, and the `phase` event carries the new `spd`.
  - `cleanse` drops debuffs, DoTs and a daze.
  - `summonWave` calls an `Encounter.reserves` group into the boss's wave, through a `summon`
    event.
- **Reserves** (`AnchorDef.reserves` and `Encounter.reserves`) are built by
  `buildAnchorEncounter` at the anchor's own elite multiplier, with stable ids
  `e{floor}_r{group}_{i}`. They are snapshotted into `unitsInit`, so the replay knows them. They
  are not counted in the anchor's budget. Summoned units join the summoner's wave, so the Egg's
  new brood re-shields the phased Egg.
- **Authored phases:**

  | Boss | At | Title | What changes |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Halgiraf | 50 % | "Takes flight" | 40 % guard against close combat (a new `GuardSource` `'melee'`), Sky Dive, +15 % speed |
  | El Cid | 50 % | "No longer holding back" | ×1.3 damage for good, +25 % speed, cleanse |
  | Valention | 50 % | "The Iron Guard" | recalls Rodvick and Lazenca (reserve `officers`), one aegis |
  | Pryos | 66 % and 33 % | "The Second Seal" / "The Last Seal" | two aegis each, +15 % speed at the last |
  | Kthat | 40 % | "The Water God stirs" | — |
  | Versace | 50 % | "Silver Lightning" | +30 % speed |
  | The Egg | 50 % | "The shell cracks" | calls `brood2` |
  | the Herald | 50 % | "The Last Word" | two aegis, End of Days, +15 % speed |
  | Tell | 75 / 50 / 25 % | "The First / Second / Last Draft" | summons `echo1` (Echo of Halgiraf), `echo2` (El Cid, Valention) and `echo3` (Pryos, the Herald) |

  The five echo templates get spectral sprites (`enemySprite.echoOf`).
- **In the replay** (`PhaseCinematic.tsx`):
  - The screen flashes.
  - A title card slams down: the boss's name, "PHASE 2 OF 2 · TAKES FLIGHT", and its line.
  - The caption names the turn.

  Under reduced motion there is no flash, and the card fades. Summoned foes march in as a wave
  does.
- **The mission HUD and turn order stay right.**
  - `turnOrder.ts` replays wind-ups as turns, holds a charging gauge, releases the move at the
    start of its tick before the fill, and names it in the strip.
  - Summons join with an empty gauge. A phase's `spd` replaces the base speed.
  - An Unleash fills the gauge before the tick's fill, as the engine does.
  - **The real-log test stays at 0 divergences.** Its strip check now also stops at a wind-up, a
    phase, a summon, a stun or a speed status. Those are the same kind of unforeseeable change
    as a death. A hero's silent turn with no foe on the field counts as unforeseeable too.

### 4. Orders 2.0

- **New `BattleOrder` kinds** (`combat.ts applyOrder`):
  - **`unleash` `{allyId}`:** the hero's gauge fills to a whole turn, and it casts its best
    *paid* skill this tick.
  - **`guard` `{onTelegraph?}`:** the party braces (above). While it braces, heroes hold their
    blows. They may still heal, shield or taunt, and otherwise spend the turn on a `brace`. The
    `onTelegraph` form is a standing order that raises the guard the moment a foe winds up.
  - **`hold`:** for the rest of the fight, SP blows go only to a sweep over three or more foes
    or to a blow on the boss. Heals are still allowed. The boss means a defeat or capture target,
    a phased boss, or a foe with a charged move.
  - **`swap` `{a, b}`:** two heroes trade lines and their order in the line, so the front-most
    one is struck first. On the stage they trade places.
- **Focus marks** make a sweep land on the mark at no less than 60 % of its force (falloff
  waived down to that floor). The brain's estimate agrees.
- **Overlook slots** become free pre-battle Protects: `FocusDirective.overlookedAllyIds`, clamped
  to `tacticalOverlookSlots(level)` by `tactical/orders.clampDirective` in `prepareFloorBattle`.
  The forecast and the attempt agree.
- **Pre-battle mark:**
  - `FocusDirective.focusEnemyId` is free and carries the Tactical Center's bonus.
  - The war room's new **"Before the fight"** panel (`tower/PreBattleOrders.tsx`) holds the mark
    as a select, the Protects as chips, and the floor's big moves and phases ("⚠ Halgiraf winds
    up Dragon Breath, Sky Dive — Guard answers it. Changes once: at 50 % — Takes flight.").
  - The forecast panel can "⌖ Mark it" on the deadliest foe or the boss (a small hook).
  - TowerScreen passes the directive to `attemptFloorWithResult`, `ATTEMPT_FLOOR.focus` and
    `store.revise`, keyed to the floor and attempt like the opening orders.
- **Order refill:** `ORDERS.refillPerWave` = 1. The rules live in `tactical/orders.ts`:
  - An order at tick t may spend what the waves cleared before t gave back.
  - `playFloor` validates after the fight with `ordersFit`. This is deterministic: the log up to
    each order's tick is the one it was given on.
  - The battle screen counts with `ordersInHand`.
- **UI:**
  - `OrderBar.tsx` shows Focus, Protect, Unleash, Guard, Hold, Swap and Retreat, with icons and
    keys F/P/U/G/H/X/R. On a phone it becomes a four-column grid of 44 px tiles.
  - Aims are shared through `ordersPlan.ts`: Unleash and Swap never aim at the escort, and a swap
    takes two clicks with the first hero highlighted.
  - The keyboard help lists every battle key.
- **Forecast:**
  - The odds-changers offer a **free mark** on the deadliest foe. This was an opening Focus that
    spent an order; that is still offered when the plan already marks someone else.
  - They also offer a **standing Guard** wherever a foe has a charged move. Like every
    alternative, it shows only when it really changes the odds.
- **Equivalence:** every kind keeps `reduce(state, cmd)` identical to
  `attemptFloorWithResult(pre, …, orders)`, tested per kind at F20 (`tactical/orders.test.ts`).

## Decisions

- **Fixed-tick telegraphs, not "fires on its next turn".** The countdown means something, the
  Master can time a Guard, and the turn order can name the fire exactly. A stun cancels rather
  than delays, because the brief asks for answers that work.
- **Phases clamp the blow** at their threshold, so every authored phase plays (no one-shot skips
  a cinematic). This costs at most one overkilled blow of HP per phase.
- **Guard holds attacks but not support.** That is the price of the brace, and a healer still
  heals. The `brace` turn is an `act`, so the turn order and the replay read it as a turn.
- **The free mark replaces the opening Focus** as the forecast's odds-changer. It is strictly
  better for the player (it spends no order). The opening Focus stays for a plan that already
  marks someone else.
- **Refill one order per cleared wave** keeps the Master's voice alive through multi-wave anchors
  (the brief's optional knob). The engine validates it post hoc.
- **The bane on Inquisitors** treats heroes as `humanoid` only for an enemy's bane. No hero has a
  humanoid bane, so PvP is untouched.
- **No budget retune.** Progression stayed close, and the boss fights got richer without
  getting harder on average. Lane R owns the shared tuning.
