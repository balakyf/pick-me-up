# Lane K · Results, memorial & transitions: notes

> **Status:** done · **Date:** 2026-10-04 · **Base:** `8be101c` (wave 5 merged) · **Branch:** `worktree-agent-ae7e68be90be49f52`
> **Brief:** pillars 4 and 5: the moments around a fight land. The results screen celebrates or mourns, rewards are celebrated, the fallen are remembered, and scenes change with a curtain.
> The worktree was created from `main` (`63c30a4`, before the waves). The branch was moved to `claude/fervent-cori-u00e2v` (`8be101c`) before any work, so this lane builds on waves 1–5.

## What shipped

### 1 · The results ceremony (`ui/results/`)

`ResultsScreen.tsx` is rebuilt as a staged ceremony. The timeline is pure (`ceremony.ts`): `ceremonyPlan(input, { speed, reduced })` returns its steps (`at`, `dur`), its cues and its total; `stepAt`, `countUp` (ease-out, lands exactly) and `cuesBetween` let the screen and the tests walk the clock. `useCeremony.ts` runs the clock on animation frames and plays each cue as the clock passes it.

- **Order:** banner → the fallen (one beat per band) → gold counting up → XP → the first-clear stamp → drops → truths found → skill milestones → the report.
- **Sound (lane H's cues):** `coins` rattles while the gold counts (up to 9 ticks), `confirm` for the stamp, `flip` per drop (`rare` for a rare one), `rare` per truth, `levelup` per skill level and `legend` for a merge or achievement. When someone fell, every cue is quieter, and nothing plays over a defeat. Lane H's victory and defeat jingles still play under the battle's banner.
- **Anchor floors:** a longer banner, a kicker ("Anchor floor · Subjugation"), the boss brought down in its title-card colour ("THE CHIMERA MATRIARCH · MOTHER OF MONSTERS has fallen", from lane I's `BOSS_INTRO`, the mission's own target first), and a gold frame on a clean anchor clear. Filler floors show their seeded name ("Floor 54 · Hall of the Burning Seal").
- **Skip:** a click on the card, **Space** or **Esc** jumps to the end, and the Skip button does the same. The main button always leaves, even mid-ceremony, so the existing smoke tests' `click('Onward')` still works. **Enter** leaves once the ceremony is over. Key repeats are ignored, so the Enter that closed the battle never skips the results.
- **Pace:** the Settings window's default battle speed divides every duration. **Reduced motion** shows everything at once, with no count-up, no entrances and at most one coin sound.
- **Reward juice:** a "+N" pops off the gold once it has counted; drops are chips with rarity glows (`rewards.ts`: the Book of Reverse Heaven is legendary and pulses, an anchor's authored drop is rare, attribute stones are uncommon). The FIRST CLEAR stamp slams onto the card's corner. Waiting beats take no room, so the card grows as it plays; the gold/XP row and the stamp keep their room so the numbers don't jump.

### 2 · The battle report (`battleStats.ts`, pure)

`battleStats(log)` reads one `CombatLog` and changes nothing in the engine:

- Per hero: **damage dealt** (HP that came off; overkill is not counted) and **taken**, **healing** (HP actually restored to the party; lifesteal goes to the striker), **shielded** (the soak of shields they cast), **kills** (the last blow or DoT pulse before a foe's death), **crits**, **skills cast** (not basic attacks, braces or charged blows), **big moves broken** (a stun or kill that cancelled a wind-up), and **orders that named them** (protect, unleash, swap).
- For the party: totals, **big moves answered / wound up** (broken, or met braced or protected, from lane G's `answered`), and orders used by kind.
- **MVP:** `mvpParts` scores in HP the party gained. Damage and healing count 1:1, shield soak 0.8, blows taken 0.35 (a tank's work), and each kill or broken big move is worth 3% / 15% of the fight's total damage. Ties go to the hero fielded first. The first version scored shares of each total, and the browser check showed why that was wrong: a 17 HP token heal (100% of the party's healing) outranked 1,500 damage. `mvpReason` captions it ("led the attack", "kept the party standing", "broke the big moves"…).
- The fallen are marked ☠ and greyed in the report.

### 3 · The memorial band (`memorialBand.ts`)

When heroes fall, the results become a memorial: `.overlay.results.grey` turns the world behind grey (`backdrop-filter`), and each fallen hero gets a band in the order they fell. The band shows their bust (greyscale), name, star and level, "climbed to floor N · served N days · mourned by …", their last words, and a line from Isel. Isel closes the band ("The floor is ours, Master. It was paid for." / "Come home, Master…").

- **One source of truth:** the grave is the Memorial's own `FallenRecord` (life/react.ts raises it with the mourners, the best floor and the days served), and the last words come from `lastWordsTogether` over the same deaths the battle's death moment used. A test pins the band against the battle's words and the Memorial's `lastWords` (via the grave row). Nothing in `engine/life` or `ui/life` changed.
- **Isel's lines** (`ISEL_FOR`: 5 for the mourned, 3 for those nobody knew well yet, 1 for a hero who served less than a day; `ISEL_CLOSE` by mood) are picked by a hash of the hero and never repeat over one battle's dead while the lines last. These are placeholders that lane M's eulogy can replace.
- **Bittersweet:** a victory with losses mourns *before* it counts. The banner's gold is tarnished, the note reads "the floor is ours — at a cost", the rewards are desaturated and the sounds are quieter. `resultMood` → `triumph | cleared | bittersweet | mourning | defeat | retreat | failed`.

### 4 · Recovery reframed, the event queue, the save error

- **Camp:** a result that opened the recovery event ends with a camp card (a two-frame pixel campfire): "Camp on the stair. The survivors make camp on the stair by floor N. Wounds are dressed, and the fallen are named by the fire." It also says what the queue holds behind it (lane B's B19 seam): "The tournament will wait until the party has recovered." / "The floor's reward waits beyond the camp." The button reads "To the camp ▸".
- **War room:** the event panel's recovery is titled **Camp**: "The party has made camp on the stair. Tend the wounded, or take in a stranger, before the next floor." Its options read "Tend the wounded by the fire…" and "A stranger at the camp asks to join…". `events.ts` gives the recovery's Rest and Reinforcement their own outcome notes. Only the note strings changed (same Sanity, same recruit, no draw moved).
- **Save error (lane B's seam):** `qol/SaveErrorNotice.tsx` subscribes to the store. On the first failed write of a run it raises a warning toast and a bar ("This browser's storage is full: your progress is not being saved. Export a copy to keep it safe." with **Export** / **Not now**). The bar clears itself when a save succeeds again. The pure half is in `qol/saveError.ts`.

### 5 · Scene transitions (`ui/transition/`)

`transition.ts` (pure) and `SceneTransition.tsx` (one component). `transitionFor(from, to, reduced)` picks the style and length: a ragged diagonal **wipe** (420 ms) between the lobby and its rooms, an **iris** (520 ms) into a battle, its results and the summoning circle, a 180 ms **fade** under reduced motion, and nothing on the first scene. `curtainGrid` and `curtainDelays` lay out the blocks (10–20 across, square-ish, row-major delays that all finish in time).

The curtain covers the new scene in a layout effect, so the scene is never seen uncovered, then its blocks shrink away in pixel steps. It is `pointer-events: none` at z-index 180 (above the battle and toasts, below the promotion ceremony and the summon ritual), and it removes itself. A module-level map keyed by `channel` remembers each mount's last scene, so the App swapping its whole tree (title → lobby) still plays a wipe.

**Decision:** reveal-only (cover instantly, then open) rather than cover-then-swap. A two-phase transition would have meant delaying `setView` in App and the Tower's battle/result state, which is more invasive in shared files for little gain.

### 6 · The title diorama (`ui/title/diorama.ts`)

The parade of seven is now a 256×144 pixel scene, scaled up crisply. It shows a night sky with a moon and two twinkling star frames, far hills, the tower rising out of the frame (drawn from `drawTowerStatic`'s dark and lit layers, lit up to F24, with its own sky and ground cut away), the waiting room with a flickering window-light layer, a path, and a campfire (two frames plus CSS embers). Three heroes keep watch by the fire and a fourth walks to the tower. It is built from the existing generators: `towerMap`, `shape`, `bitmap`, `rand`, and `heroFrameUrl` for the heroes.

**Rule 12:** six bitmaps (`dio|base`, `dio|stars|0/1`, `dio|lights`, `campfire|0/1`) are cached by fixed keys, and all motion is CSS stacking them. Reduced motion stills it and hides the embers.

## Decisions

- **The button always leaves.** Skipping is a separate gesture (a click on the card, Space or Esc), so nobody is forced to sit through a ceremony and the old tests' flow still works.
- **A victory with losses mourns first.** The fallen come before the gold, and the gold comes quieter.
- **No engine change for the stats.** Everything the report needs was already in the log (`answered`, `telegraph-end`, `status` sources, `shield`, `order`).
- **Grey is a backdrop filter** on the results overlay, so the war room behind goes grey without touching any other component.
- **The campfire bitmap is shared** between the title and the camp card (one cache key per frame).

## What is left

- **Lane M (story):** Isel's eulogy lines here are a small placeholder pool (`ISEL_FOR` / `ISEL_CLOSE`), so a written eulogy per voice, per bond or per floor can replace `iselFor` and `iselClose` without touching the screen. The anchor banner could carry the anchor's own story line once briefings exist.
- **Lane L (grief):** the band shows the grave's mourners as they stood at the death. A "mourning for N days" line, or the mourners' current grief, can be added once lane L exposes a query. A pure helper in `memorialBand.ts` would read it.
- **Transitions are reveal-only** (see §5). A true cover-then-swap would need App and TowerScreen to delay their state change. The curtain is not used by `PendingReplayHost` (a reload's replay), the event outcome card or the Daily Dungeon.
- **The results overlay is still the Tower's.** The Daily Dungeon, raids and the Echo Trial keep their own result cards, but `battleStats` and the ceremony helpers are generic and ready for them.
- **Battle speed:** the ceremony reads the Settings default, not the speed the Master switched to mid-battle (`BattleScene`'s `onDone` carries no speed). A one-line change in lane I/E's BattleScene could pass it through.
- **Diorama:** it doesn't reflect the account (the Master's real heroes, the floor reached, a dead world after F90). The title shows no account by design (it is the pre-load screen), but a "Continue" diorama could draw the save's party.
- The old `.title-parade` rules in `ui.css` are dead and can go in a `ui.css` cleanup.
