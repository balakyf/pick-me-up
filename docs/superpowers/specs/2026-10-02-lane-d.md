# Lane D · Combat brain

> **Status:** Done · **Date:** 2026-10-02 · **Base:** 0e7a09b · **Worktree branch:** `worktree-wf_c02d5bfe-14a-2`
> **Plan:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-02-dream-game.md` (pillar 3: fights are short dramas you can read and steer).

Heroes now fight like the "Quantum AI" they are, fights last long enough to be fights, enemy
casters cast, every hit says how it met its target's defences, and every battle carries its
mission so the replay can show it.

## What shipped (brief item by item)

1. **Skill choice by expected damage (O4a).** `combat.ts chooseSkill` scores every castable
   skill by the damage it can be expected to land. The estimate (`estimateBlow`) is integer
   per-mille math: attack × skill × `damageScale` × element (×1.5 / ×0.75) × mitigation × the
   expected crit, the actor's keywords (enrage, frenzy, opener, bane), the target's
   resist / vulnerable / guard, line and floor multipliers, and the sweep's falloff. Each
   target's share is capped at its current HP, so overkill and immunity count and a
   single-target skill wins on a lone boss. Ties: never pay HP for nothing, then the bigger
   raw blow (heroes practise the skills they know), then the cheaper SP. No RNG is drawn.
2. **Immunity-aware targeting (B6).** Single-target picks pass over foes immune to the
   chosen blow while another foe can be hurt (a focus order on an immune foe too; a sword
   still obeys it). A skill that would hit only immune foes scores 0. A friend never
   follows up on a foe their strike cannot hurt. At the Wall, mages stop casting into the
   magic-immune Fragment Knights.
   **Added in this pass: futility.** When nothing the party still holds can hurt any foe it
   has to beat (castable skills, since SP never refills; the basic fallback; the follow-up
   strike), the party falls back at once: outcome `retreat` and a `futile` mission beat
   ("Nothing we have can touch the Fragment Warden — fall back!"). Found in the browser at
   F81: a party of blades swung IMMUNE at a lone physical-immune Warden for over three
   minutes of replay. Never on a Survival or an Escape, which waiting or walking can win.
3. **HP-cost floor (B15).** `canCast`: an HP-cost cast needs HP after the cost ≥
   `TUNING.combat.hpCostFloorPct` (30 %) of max HP.
4. **The AoE monopoly.** A sweep over n foes lands ×100 / (100 + k(n − 1)) on each
   (`TUNING.combat.aoeFalloffK` 60: 2 foes ×0.63 each, 4 ×0.36, 8 ×0.19). Class-gating
   Incident was not needed. **`TUNING.combat.damageScale` 0.58**: every blow lands at 58 % of
   the formula, so a late fight lasts about three rounds a side. It changes the pace only:
   HP, CP and budgets stay as they are. Raid pools follow (`CHALLENGE.raids.hpMult` 4 → 2.4).
   The acceptance gate passes (numbers below).
5. **Enemy casters (B5).** An explicit `caster: true` on 11 INT-built templates (Dark
   Disciple, Black Priest, Lizardman Shaman, XYZ Mage Golem, Kurushahr, Crystal Core, Order
   Battlemage / Inquisitor / Saint, Wraith, Fragment Warden). `unit.ts enemyBasicAttack`
   gives them a magic Spell (`e_spell`, mAtk vs mDef). The replay calls it "Spell" and it
   flies as a bolt (`choreo.ts`).
6. **Hit effectiveness.** `'hit'` events carry `eff: 'weak' | 'resist' | 'immune'` (element
   wheel, vulnerable, resist keyword; guards are armour and get no tag). An immune blow is
   never flagged critical: the roll is still spent, so the stream does not change.
   `DamagePopups.popupLook` (pure, tested) shows IMMUNE instead of "CRITICAL! 0", plus WEAK! /
   RESIST tags (`battle/hitEffect.css`).
7. **Follow-ups (B32).** `resolveHit(…, followUp = true)`: a friend's pressed strike neither
   uses nor spends their `opener`, so the bonus lands on their own first action only.
8. **Mission events (O7 engine half, B37).**
   - `CombatLog.mission`: `{ type, objectives (+ unitIds carrying each tag), timerTicks?, waves }`.
     It is built in `combat.ts` from `encounter.mission`, so `tower.ts` needed no edit.
   - `CombatUnitInit.spd` (always written) and `targetTag` (objective units only).
   - `'mission'` events gain optional `code` / `params`. The `note` stays, so the change is
     backwards compatible. Codes: `wave-cleared`, `hold` (survival quarters), `deadline`
     (timer quarters), `escape` (quarters of the distance), `taken`, `defeated`,
     `escort-low` (below 50 % / 25 %), `shield-down`, `wakes`, `horde-spent`, `futile`. No RNG.
   - A Survival whose waves are spent and foes dead is won at once ("the horde is spent").
   - `maybeAdvanceWave` no longer re-counts a last wave that a looming unit stands in.
   - UI: `battle/missionCaptions.ts` (the synergyCaptions pattern) gives each code a caption
     and a duration. `battleFrames.eventDuration(e)` feeds BattleScene, and that is its only edit.
9. **Rebalance & golden.** Budgets were retuned (table in the balance-pass doc). The golden
   combat test moved on purpose, with damageScale as the reason: eventCount 9 → 15 (Han needs
   four swings, and the Goblin answers twice), rngDraws 6 → 12 (6 hits × 2 draws; the brain
   draws nothing), ticksElapsed 29 → 58 (the fight lasts twice as long), outcome and
   wavesCleared unchanged. There is a dated section in
   `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-29-balance-pass.md`.

French: `src/ui/i18n/slices/frCombatBrain.ts` (IMMUNE, WEAK!, RESIST, Spell, every mission caption).

`npx vite-node src/sim/combatMetrics.ts [days] [seeds] [fromFloor] [out.json]` plays the
same bots and seeds as `npm run sim` and reads back every tower attempt (`sim/fightStats.ts`,
pure and tested). Replay length uses BattleScene's own timings. "Before" was measured in a

but read worse over 3. The 3-seed draw happens to include the two engaged runs whose Act V–VI
got deadlier (23 → 58 and 29 → 58 dead), while seeds 3–5 went the other way (76 → 17,

hits now strike a weakness, wasted immune hits stay near 0.

## Decisions

- **Falloff plus damageScale instead of class-gating Incident.** Falloff spreads a sweep's
  force, but it does not make a fight longer: a party with full-strength blows still ended a
  late fight in about one round (the "1.2 rounds" before). Lowering every blow fixes the pace
  without touching HP or CP, so forecasts, CP and the budgets' meaning stay intact.
  Class-gating would have taken Incident away from classes that learn it in canon.
- **Mission metadata comes from `encounter.mission` inside `runBattle`.** Lane C is
  restructuring `tower.ts`, so this lane did not touch it.
- **Futility ends as `retreat`**, the outcome a Master's retreat order already produces
  (the results screen says RETREATED and nothing new is needed). The state can only get
  worse, so ending early changes nothing except the deaths the stalemate would have caused.
- **The Wall at 2.3**: the smallest step that holds it for 6/6 whales. Pass 2's intent was
  "the gate holds".

## Left for later lanes

- **Casual deaths per attempt went up** (0.69 → 0.78): casual never retreats. That belongs to
  the safety rails (lane C / a later balance pass), not to the budgets.
- **Late replays are long at 1×** (F61–79 median 67 s, p90 108 s). Lane E could trim beat
  timings or default long fights to 2×.
- **Phone readability** of WEAK!/RESIST over sweeps, and an objective HUD that reads
  `log.mission` (codes and params are ready). Both are lane E. Lane C's forecast can read
  `log.mission` too.
- ~~Futility does not catch a hurtable phased foe shielded by an immune wavemate~~ — fixed in
  the review (below).
- `CombatUnitInit.spd` is ready for a turn-order strip.

## Review (adversarial pass, 2026-10-02)

Checked the diff `0e7a09b..HEAD` against every brief item, re-ran typecheck, vitest, the
metrics gate (`npx vite-node src/sim/combatMetrics.ts 30 1`: all four criteria pass) and
`npm run sim -- 30 3` (finals and deaths identical to the lane's own run), and replayed F5
(1280×800), F45 (390×844), F80 (390×844) and F81 (1280×800) on port 5284: IMMUNE / WEAK! /
RESIST tags, mission captions ("Wave 1 of 2 cleared!", "The horde is spent", escape quarters,
the futile fall-back), caster Spells, no page errors. Fixed:

- **Two skill-learning tests had been weakened for nothing.** `daily.test.ts` and
  `tower.test.ts` were moved to deeper floors with `level >= 2` instead of `toBe(2)`, with a
  comment ("the AI keeps its SP when a plain blow would kill") that the code contradicts: on a
  tie the AI takes the bigger blow. The original tests pass unchanged, so they are restored.
- **Futility counted mission NPCs' skills.** An escort never strikes, but a magic skill in its
  list kept a blades-only party swinging IMMUNE until the clock ran out. Only the party's own
  heroes count now (test added).
- **Futility missed a phased foe shielded by an immune wavemate** (listed as left for later).
  A phased foe the party could hurt now counts as out of reach while a non-phased wavemate the
  party cannot hurt still shields it (test added; a hurtable shield keeps the fight going).
- The skill-choice comment said ties go to the cheaper skill; the code gives them to the bigger
  uncapped blow (then the cheaper one). The comment now says what the code does. Removed the
  unused `estimateHit`.

Looked at and left as is: on a tie (both blows kill) the AI spends SP on the bigger skill rather
than keeping it for a later wave. It is deliberate (skills level by use) and pinned by a test.
A sweep counts immune foes in its falloff (each IMMUNE soaks its share), which is why a late
Wall fight can show many IMMUNE popups from sweeps.
