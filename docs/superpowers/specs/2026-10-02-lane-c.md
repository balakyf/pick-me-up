# Lane C · War room — notes

> **Status:** done · **Date:** 2026-10-02 · **Base:** `0e7a09b` · **Brief:** the simulated forecast, safety sheets, orders that behave, replay on reload, the Tower screen as a war room. Bug ids (B…) and opportunity ids (O…) refer to the research synthesis behind `2026-10-02-dream-game.md`.

No death is an ambush any more. Before every floor the crystal runs the real fight and says what it saw. Enter asks when something is wrong and names who and why. Orders reach the escort and skip nothing. A reload mid-battle shows the fight instead of applying it silently.

## What shipped

### 1 · The forecast engine (B1, O3)
- **`tower.prepareFloorBattle(state, { focus, ballista, subvert, orders })`** (`src/engine/tower/tower.ts`) builds exactly the battle an attempt fights. That covers the party through the deploy rails (morale, refusals, the rebellion draw), the real `buildEncounter` (anchor and Wall scaling, loop scars, floor conditions), the ballista wound, the subversion, the orders, the bonds, the set bonuses and the Shrine's blessing, and the attempt's `combatSeed`. It returns those inputs plus the practised meta. `playFloor` now calls it and validates nothing new. Every existing tower test is unchanged and still green.
- **`src/engine/scout/forecast.ts`** runs that prepared battle `FORECAST.runs` = 16 times (tuning in `scout/forecastTuning.ts`). The seeds are `hash(seed, 'forecast', floor, attemptIndex, i)` and never the attempt's `'combat'` seed, so the forecast predicts without spoiling the real fight. A `Forecast` holds:
  - win %, the outcome mix (win, wipe, failed, timeout, retreat) and expected deaths (one decimal);
  - one row per slotted hero: fights or stays home and the true reason, Sanity, death % across the runs, panic % per turn, under the low-Sanity line;
  - the median battle length;
  - the deadliest enemy: most effective damage to the party, plus its wave, share and kills per run, and whether it is a sleeping looming giant;
  - the boss (a defeat/acquire target) with its median HP left in the losses, and each escort's fall %;
  - the mission as data, enemy count and waves, floor modifiers, party CP against the CP of the encounter actually built, and the opening orders;
  - **a threat band from the forecast**: Safe is ≥ 90% won and < 0.1 deaths; Fair is ≥ 70% and < 0.75; Risky is > 40% and < 2; everything else is Deadly. The band names are kept and the CP-ratio bands are gone (`threatFor(winPct, expectedDeaths)`). `ScoutReport` dropped `threat`/`expectedDeaths`, and its `budget` is now the ΣCP of the built encounter.
- **What would change the odds** (`forecastAlternatives`): up to three plans, each re-forecast and shown only when it really helps (≥ 5 points more wins, or ≥ 0.25 fewer deaths, best first). When nobody on the board can fight, every plan that fields someone is shown, even a grim one.
  1. the suggested party;
  2. the unfit, refusing or under-30-Sanity heroes swapped for the strongest rested bench heroes the rails would send, with empty slots filled the same way;
  3. an opening **Focus** on the deadliest enemy. This is a `focus` order at tick 1, so it uses one of the battle's orders and carries the Tactical Center bonus. It is never offered against a looming enemy.
- **`suggestParty`** counter-picks once at least a quarter of the floor shrugs off a damage type. Immune counts 1 and resist ½, and the boss is weighted ×3 (`readFloor`). It also favours heroes of the boss's weakness (×1.3) or element advantage over it (×1.15), and ranks by true CP. For example, at the Wall it now brings blades and Light.
- `enterConcerns(f)` names what the Enter sheet must ask about: fewer than 5 fight, anyone stays home, anyone under 30 Sanity, or ≤ 40% / Deadly.
- **Memo:** the key is an FNV hash and length of the exact battle input (seed, floor, attempt, runs, prepared units, encounter, refusals, party), kept in a 48-entry LRU. The UI also keeps alternatives per key and pre-caches each plan's own forecast, so "Use this party" shows its numbers at once.

**Cost.** Node timings per forecast (16 runs) on the bot saves, with the suggested party at each floor:

| save | F10 | F40 | F80 | its own floor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| engaged 3d | 4 ms | 8 ms | 1 ms | F39: 8 ms |
| engaged 10d | 2 ms | 3 ms | 2 ms | F60: 7 ms |
| engaged 25d | 2 ms | 3 ms | 2 ms | F80: 4 ms (as-is) |
| whale 20d | 1 ms | 1 ms | 2 ms | F80: 1 ms (as-is) |

Long survival floors are the worst case, because cost grows with ticks: F5 at 1,200 ticks takes 54 ms and F65 at 1,000 ticks takes 18–49 ms. The alternatives cost about as much again. A cache hit costs 0.2–0.5 ms, which is the prepare plus the hash.

Because the worst case passes ~60 ms (and a 5,000-tick timeout would cost several times that), the forecast runs in a **Web Worker**: `src/ui/tower/forecast.worker.ts`, created with `new Worker(new URL(…), { type: 'module' })`. If the browser refuses a worker, a chunked fallback runs about 8 ms of battles per `setTimeout`. With no Worker API at all (jsdom), it runs at once. A busy worker computing a plan nobody wants is replaced.

Browser-measured with Playwright: the dev and production builds both load `forecast.worker`, the forecast appears 120–170 ms after page load, and no long task comes from it.

### 2 · ForecastPanel (`src/ui/tower/ForecastPanel.tsx`)
- A big win % and the band, plus the outcome bar ("cleared 14/16 · wiped 2/16" on hover) and the expected deaths in words. It also shows "16 runs of the real fight · median N ticks · party X vs floor Y".
- One risk chip per hero, with the name disambiguated by lane A's `pickerName`. Each chip reads like "Bram · falls in 81%", "Islat stays home: is burnt out and resting" (the tooltip gives the fix from `deployReason.ts`), "Zarek · may panic: Sanity 12", or "Dorn comes home".
- The objective in plain words ("Hold out for 900 ticks.", "Keep Princess Priasis alive — if they fall, the mission fails.") and the escort's odds.
- The deadliest enemy and its wave, and the boss's HP left when the party lost.
- The old scouting report's roster is kept: keywords, Codex weaknesses, "study this floor", and the immunity warnings.
- The odds-changers have a one-click "Use this party", which dispatches `SET_PARTY`, or "Use this plan" for the opening Focus. Once adopted, the plan shows "⚑ Opening order: Focus Assassin · cancel".
- "Consulting the crystal…" shows while the crystal is working, and "The crystal weighs other plans…" while only the alternatives are pending.

### 3 · Enter confirm (O2 UI, B20) — `src/ui/tower/EnterConfirm.tsx`
- Enter opens the sheet only for the brief's concerns: a short party, a refusal, Sanity < 30, or ≤ 40% / Deadly. The sheet names each hero and why, with the fix, then gives the odds. Its buttons are **Back** (which has focus; Escape also goes back) and **Enter anyway**.
- A healthy, safe party enters with one click. If the forecast is still pending, Enter computes it on the spot.
- **F90 always asks.** The sheet says what clearing the floor does ("ends the world beneath the tower…"), how many truths the Master knows, and whether they can still refuse. When earned, it offers **Subvert ✦**, and explains that Subvert strips the Herald's aegis and spares the world. Otherwise it says how many truths are still needed, or that too many lie behind.

### 5 · Orders
- **B3:** Protect can be aimed at the escort, on the stage and in the party window. The hint reads "Click a hero or the escort…". Test: an escort Protect order on the casual bot's F15 keeps `reduce` deep-equal to `attemptFloorWithResult`, and no enemy aims at her afterwards while a hero stands.

A jsdom test fails on the old logic (the HP bar jumped).

### 6 · Replay on reload (B14)
- `src/ui/tower/pendingReplay.ts` writes `localStorage['pmu.pendingReplay']` before the dispatch. The record holds the log once, the result, the floor, `savedAt`, the account id and the tower position the attempt leaves. Orders (`revise`) update it.
- Dismissing the results clears it, and so do New game (Erase) and import.
- On load, `PendingReplayHost` (mounted by the App) replays the fight under the banner "⟲ The battle you left on floor N — it was fought; here is how it went.", including its death moments, then shows the results. A record that does not match the save (another account, an older save, a crash between write and dispatch) is dropped unseen.
- **No spoilers behind the overlay:** while a battle and its results play, TowerScreen renders the pre-attempt state (`frozen`), including its cached forecast. Before this, the page behind already read "Recovery — the main team is gone".

### 7 · The Tower as a war room (O22, B26)
- TowerScreen is now a composition of `src/ui/tower/*`:
  - the tower art;
  - a **sticky command panel** (`CommandPanel`): the tower's banners, the open event and its queue ("⏭ Then: Tournament", from lane B's `eventQueue`), the forecast, the **party strip** with five Sanity pips per hero and a glyph for why anyone stays home, **Enter** (stuck to the bottom of the column, so it never scrolls out of reach), the side door and challenges, and the bonds;
  - the floor column (`FloorList`, `Chronicle`).
- Layout: three columns at desktop width, two below 1180 px, one on a phone. At ≥ 1600×900 the tower art scales to 3×.
- **Acts beyond the current one** are sealed: "🔒 Act VI — ??? · F70–79 · not yet reached". Nothing ahead is named.
- **Seeded floors** get deterministic names such as "Stair of Wolfsong" or "Vault of Black Glass" (`floorNames.ts`). Eight nouns × six qualifiers per act cover the largest act, there are no repeats within an act, and the shuffle is per account and act. The Wall's names are the same for every Master. Each floor also gets a mission icon from its real encounter (memoized).
- **Chronicle:** states the truth rule ("A Master who knows 7 of the tower's truths before the ninetieth floor may refuse what it asks."), then how many more are needed or that it is out of reach. It tallies found, ahead and missed, and marks missed truths.
- **TowerExterior (B26):** two cached layers per crack stage (dark, and fully lit clipped at the highest floor) plus a CSS marker that glides by transition. That is at most about 120 cached images per game, and in practice about 20. Before, it was about 3 canvases and a `toDataURL` per climb step.
- **SynergyPanel** names pairs by disambiguated names (no more "Maren & Maren").

## Decisions
- **Seeds:** forecast runs use `hash(seed, 'forecast', floor, attemptIndex, i)`. This is the `rngFor` stream the brief names, in the numeric form `runBattle` takes. The attempt's own `'combat'` seed is never read, so the forecast is a distribution, never a spoiler.
- **The forecast reads the Party Board.** Alternatives are applied by dispatching `SET_PARTY` without a clock, so the adopted plan's cached forecast matches at once.
- **The opening Focus is an order at tick 1, not a pre-battle `FocusDirective`.** It counts against the Tactical Center's order allowance (the battle shows one fewer), and it appears in the replay as the Master's order.
- **Enter-sheet conditions follow the brief exactly.** A Risky floor that still wins more than 40% enters without a sheet, but its risk chips and expected deaths are on screen.
- **The pending replay is UI-side**, with no schema change. The attempt is still dispatched first, so a reload can never undo a death.
- **The command panel scrolls inside itself on desktop**, and Enter sticks to its bottom. On a phone the page scrolls and Enter sticks to the bottom of the viewport.
- **Test changes:**
  - The **scout test's band test** changed: the old CP-ratio bands are gone by design.
  - The **app smoke test** now expects sealed acts. "Act VIII — The Unfinished Floors" at F6 was exactly the spoiler the brief removes.

## What is left

In the 3-day save at F39, the plain bench swap (0.4 deaths) beats the CP-ranked suggestion (2.7 deaths), which suggests a forecast-ranked suggestion (try a few candidate parties) would be a cheap win now that it runs in a worker.

## Review
An adversarial pass over `0e7a09b..HEAD`. I checked it against the brief, ran typecheck and the full suite, and ran the sim (`30 3`: 22 s, numbers as above). I also browser-checked at 1280×800 and 390×844 with fresh bot saves: a casual F15 escort floor, engaged 10d at F68, engaged 25d at F77, and a crafted F90 with 7 truths. On F15 I gave a Protect order to Priasis, reloaded mid-battle, then watched the replay and the results.

What held:
- `prepareFloorBattle` is the one input for both the attempt and the forecast, and every tower test is unchanged.
- The forecast seeds never read `'combat'`.
- The memo key covers everything `runBattle` reads.
- B18 only adds `focusBonus` when a Focus order exists. No golden value moved.
- Protect on the escort keeps `reduce` ≡ `attemptFloorWithResult`.
- The pending replay is written before the dispatch and matched by account and tower position.
- Nothing behind the overlay spoils the outcome.
- No French strings are missing: I scanned every `t()`/`tn()` literal in the changed UI files.

Fixed:
- **Subvert showed the wrong odds.** The room's *Subvert ✦* button opened the Enter sheet with the forecast of the plain clear, where the Herald keeps her aegis. A crafted F90 read "38% — Deadly" when the subverted fight is 100%. `enter(true)` now forecasts `{ subvert: true }`. The world-ending sheet, which also offers Subvert, now shows the subverted odds beside the plain ones ("Subverted, the crystal gives this party N% — band."; French added). New smoke test, which fails on the old code.
- **The replay note hid the battle caption.** "⟲ The battle you left…" sat on top of the stage's caption bar on desktop. It now sits just below it, with a smaller placement on phones.
- **The tower marker's fall.** The comment promised an instant fall back down (the F40 loop), but the CSS transition animated it too. A fall now jumps.
- **Test hygiene.** `warRoom.smoke.test.tsx` never unmounted its Apps, so old clocks ticked into later tests ("not wrapped in act" noise). It now unmounts after each test.

Left as is (judged fine):
- The forecast bots dare once a week has passed on a floor, then keep daring every session until it clears. This matches the old CP-ratio "patient" rule, and they retreat when it goes badly (RETREAT = FORECAST_DARED).
- Seeded floor names repeat a qualifier within an act ("Vault of Red Stone", "Landing of Red Stone"). The pairs are unique, as the notes say.
- The forecast panel itself shows the plain clear at F90. The subverted odds appear on the sheets.
