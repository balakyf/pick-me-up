# Lane B · Foundation engine — notes

> **Status:** done · **Date:** 2026-10-02 · **Base:** `16394db` · **Brief:** deploy rails, true CP, gear on death, banquet, persistence, personality cache, Master Level curve, engine bug sweep, sim.

The engine no longer betrays the player in the places it silently did: nobody is sent to a floor they can't fight, a refusal names its real cause, the dead don't keep the armory's best blade, the banquet table is not a favor mill, the save survives a full disk without a 1 Hz hitch, and the late systems gated behind Master Level are now reachable inside the climb.

## What shipped

### 1 · Deploy rails (B2, engine half of O2)
- **`src/engine/tower/deploy.ts`** — `fitToDeploy(state, hero, { rebellion? })` → `{ ok: true } | { ok: false, reason }` with reasons `dead · captive · expedition · promotion · training · bounty · burnout · exhausted (Sanity 0) · rebellion`. One source of truth: the estate's bounty/burnout check is `estateRefusal()` (estate/deploy.ts; `refusesDeploy` and `estateBusy` read it), rebellion is the tower's own seeded draw (`rebelsNow`, `rngFor(seed, 'rebel', floor, attemptIndex, heroId)`, gated on a positive chance — replays unchanged). The estate reason wins over "exhausted" (it is the one the Master can act on).
- `deployParty(state, build, opts, slots?, lines?)` walks slots and returns `{ units, ids, refusals }`; `heroUnfitReason(hero)` is the hero-only half; `canDeploy`, `fitCount`.
- **`deployReport(state)`** — one row per party slot `{ slot, heroId, line, fit, reason?, sanity, sanityMax }` (`reason: 'empty'` for an empty slot). Pure. The rebellion draw is fixed before Enter, so the report says exactly what the attempt will do — the coming Enter sheet can name each hero who stays home.
- **`playFloor`** rides the rails (it used to field heroes mid-promotion) and **throws `playFloor: no one is fit to fight`** when nobody deploys: no attempt index spent, no F40 loop attempt burned, no "wipe of nobody". `FloorResult.refusals: { heroId, reason }[]` lists every slotted hero who stayed home; `refusedHeroIds` keeps its old meaning (rebellion, burnout, bounty).
- Every other place that decides who fights uses it: the Daily Dungeon (now leaves the promoting/bounty-bound home), scout `partyHeroes` (rebels excluded) and `canFight(h, state?)`, `suggestParty`, `pvpReady(state, h)` (B40: bounty/burnout), guild raid/server war, raids (`fitToFight(h, state?)`), side rooms, the tournament, the weekly trial (`canEnterTrial(h, state?)`: away/busy heroes stay out; low Sanity and burnout don't matter in the Crack's simulation), tryout duels, the Party Board's `deployable`, Isel's advice.
- `src/ui/deployReason.ts` — `deployReasonText(reason)` ("is burnt out and resting") and `deployReasonFix(reason)` ("Let them rest.") for the Enter sheet and the results screen (B13's root cause: one reason per refusal). French in `frFoundation.ts`.

### 2 · True CP (B23)
- **`src/engine/unit/trueCp.ts`** — `heroUnitFull / heroCpFull / heroStatsFull(ctx, hero)` build the unit exactly as the tower does (`buildCombatUnit` with the inventory, then the estate's `moraleAdjust`): gear, passive and engraving %, favor, the Sanity penalty, withdrawal. Test pins it to the CP the tower actually fields.
- Scout: `heroCp(h, state?)` is true CP; `partyCp(state, heroes)` adds the party's bond-set bonuses and the Shrine's blessing; `suggestParty` ranks by it (memoized per call). **The scout's threat bands shift**: a geared, Bonded party reads stronger, a tired or Wary one weaker — the bands were calibrated on the old number and the war-room lane (C) re-derives them anyway.
- UI: `cpOf(hero, state?)` (bits.tsx) reads true CP; without a state it uses the live store's account when this very hero object belongs to it, so untouched call sites (screens.tsx) are truthful too. `statsOf` feeds the HeroCard stat grid. Party Board, Raid planner and Weekly trial pass the state.

### 3 · Gear on the dead (B7) and EVA/ACC (B42)
- `equippedItemIds` and `equipItem` ignore dead holders — **this repairs existing saves on read, no migration**. Every death site (tower, raid, side rooms, synthesis, a captor's deadline) empties the fallen's hands (`releaseGear`). An Oath-Blade bound to a hero who has fallen can be passed on (`boundElsewhere`, `wieldable`, `wearerOf`). The anvil refuses nothing new.
- `FallenRecord.carried?` records what they carried; `heirlooms(state, grave)` tells who wields it now. The Memorial panel shows "Carried {item} — now in {name}'s hands." / "— it waits in the armory."
- B42: no item source ever rolled EVA/ACC (the research read `STAT_KEYS` as rolls) and a test now pins that for every slot × grade; `combatSubstats()` folds any legacy `evaPct/accPct` on an item into SPD/CRIT (`TUNING.lobby.equipment.legacySubstats`).

### 4 · Banquet (B8)
- Once per world-day: `banquet: the hall is still being cleaned`. Favor only when no banquet in the previous `favorGapDays` (3) world-days; Sanity always restores. Optional `meta.banquetDay` (no schema bump). Helpers: `banquetRefusal`, `banquetReady`, `banquetReadyAt`, `banquetWarms`. 30 daily feasts now warm the roster once (+2) instead of 30 times (+60); spaced every 4 days, 8 times. Isel's advice and the bots wait for the hall.

### 5 · Persistence (B9 + store half of O29)
- `createStore` persists a `TICK` only every `TUNING.persistence.tickPersistEvery` (15) ticks (and only if the tick changed anything); every other command persists at once. `store.flush()` writes what is pending; `useGame` calls it on `visibilitychange → hidden` and `pagehide`. `load()` marks the save current (an import is never overwritten by a stale flush).
- Writes are wrapped: a refused write (QuotaExceededError) still applies the state and notifies; `store.getSaveError()` holds the error until a write succeeds; one `console.warn`. Browser-checked: 20 s in the lobby = 1 write (was 20); hiding the page flushes.
- Isel's advice: `advise()` is memoized on `adviceSignature(state)` (engine, tested) instead of recomputing every tick.

### 6 · Personality cache (B25) and cameo pasts (B47)

Test: promotion after a first read now reads exactly what a fresh process would.

## Decisions
- Rebellion is part of `fitToDeploy` by default (tower attempts); every non-tower caller passes `{ rebellion: false }` (the draw is keyed on the tower's floor and attempt). The report reveals the draw: honest, and the Master can swap the rebel out — that is the pillar.
- `refusals` lists every slotted hero who stayed home (the dead included); `refusedHeroIds` keeps the old "refused the order" set so the current results screen keeps working.
- True CP includes the Sanity penalty and favor (exactly what combat fields), so a tired hero's CP visibly drops; the weekly trial picker still uses it although the Crack fields copies at full Sanity.
- No schema bump: `meta.banquetDay`, `tower.eventQueue`, `gift.recent`, `FallenRecord.carried` are optional with defaulting reads.
- `deployReport` returns all five slots (with `'empty'`), plus `slot` and `line`, beyond the brief's minimum.

## Adversarial review (lane B reviewer)
- **Master Level opening** — the flat `500 + 70·L` table made ML2 cost 570 XP (was 60): the Smithy, Tavern, Garden and Training Center moved from F2 to F8, the Promotion Chamber and Synthesis from F6–7 to F12–14, and First Steps' "clear a floor or two first" became false. Fixed with the three-piece table in §7 (late gates unchanged within noise); a test pins the early promise.
- **True CP cost** — `cpOf`/`heroCp` build a whole combat unit, and the Registry sorts by it inside its comparator on every render (the lobby ticks at 1 Hz): ~36 ms a render on a 466-hero whale save (old stat-only CP ~5 ms). `heroCpFull` now memoizes per (immutable) hero object, keyed on the inventory array and the withdrawn flag: 0.3 ms. Test: no stale number after a reforge or a withdrawal.
- **Old saves' Master XP** — a save made under the old curve can hold more XP than the new table's next level; `advanceTime` now always settles (`addMasterXp(meta, 0)` carries the overflow), so the gauge never reads "1900 / 1260 XP".
- **Determinism guard** — also bans Math.log2/log10/log1p/expm1/atan2/hypot/cbrt and the (inverse) hyperbolics (`\bMath\.log\b` never matched `log2`); none was in use.
- **Queued tournament on a party of the fallen** — after a heavy-loss F41 first clear the tournament now waits behind the recovery, so it can open while the party slots hold the dead; `runTournament` rightly refuses until the Master fields the living (test added), but the sim bot never re-set its party while an event was open and would stall. It now fields the fit when the tournament refuses (`npm run sim -- 30 3` is identical — it never triggered in those runs). The two "no deployable heroes" refusals (tournament, Daily Dungeon) got French.
