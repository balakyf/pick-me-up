# Lane J · Heroes are people

> **Status:** Done · **Date:** 2026-10-02 · **Base:** f282864 · **Worktree branch:** `worktree-wf_d72fc572-808-2`
> **Plan:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-02-dream-game.md` (pillar 2: heroes are people, not stat blocks; opportunities O11 and O20).

You summon a person now. Out of the crystal steps a hunter who loves apple pie and talks
cheerfully ("Pick me up! …You already did? Ha!"), Hot-Blooded, best at VIT. A 4★ arrives with
her engraving seal, her Oath-Blade and her skills stamped on the card; a canon cameo walks out
of a gilded frame to a fanfare. Promotion is a ceremony: the chamber shows exactly what will
happen before a stone is paid, lets the Master pick a calling (one of two) or a skill (one of
three), and when the timer runs out the stars burst, the grades tick up and a trait may awaken.

## What shipped (brief item by item)

### 1. Innate traits — `src/engine/content/traits.ts`

- **Twenty traits in ten families**, each with a common and a rare form:
  Brave → Lionheart (frenzy + steadier nerves), Hot-Blooded → Glass Cannon (attack up, defence
  or life down), Steadfast → Unbreakable (guard), Quick Study → Prodigy (speed, crit, faster
  self-practice), Lucky → Fortune's Child (crit; the rare one turns the first blow aside),
  Healer's Hands → Saint's Touch (lifesteal; a born healer), Spearhead → Born Leader (opener;
  a born instructor), Lone Wolf → One-Man Army (better outside a bond group), Night-Fighter →
  Moonblade (guards against shadow, bane against the restless dead and demons), Iron Stomach →
  Strong as an Ox (life, status resistance, slower hunger).
- **Derived, never stored.** The family is a weighted draw from the hero's temperament
  (`personalityOf`: courage leans Brave, temper Hot-Blooded, diligence Steadfast, curiosity
  Quick Study, warmth Healer's Hands, sociability Spearhead, its lack Lone Wolf, owls
  Night-Fighter, cooks Iron Stomach; Lucky is luck) — weight `4 + s²` with `s` the leaning on
  0..10 — or authored for the nine canon cameos (Han Steadfast, Jenna Healer's Hands, Ridigeon
  Hot-Blooded, Kishasha Lone Wolf…). Only star-independent personality fields are read, so the
  family never changes. No field, no migration, a small memo.
- **Rarity rises with the star, and only ever up.** One fixed per-hero roll (0..999) against
  `TUNING.traits.rarePerMille` = 60/120/220/380/540/700/850 ‰ for 1★…7★. A promotion can awaken
  the rare form (Brave → Lionheart: a ceremony beat) and never takes it back.
- **B25 (personality caching) was already fixed** at the base (the memo keys on every input);
  the trait reads it and adds its own fully-keyed memo.
- **Resolved in `unit.ts buildCombatUnit`** (a dozen lines): keywords join the unit's, `statPct`
  joins the engraving/passive %, `alonePct` only without a bond group, `flat` crit and
  status-resistance points after gear (crit stays capped), and a CP term
  (`TUNING.traits.cp` 6 / 14) for what keywords do that the stat line cannot show. Every combat
  unit (tower, daily, raids, PvP, duels, rooms) reads it; the forecast and true CP follow.
- **The Living Lobby, lightly:** Iron Stomach / Strong as an Ox get hungry 25 / 40 % more slowly
  (`life.ts`), Quick Study / Prodigy self-practise 50 / 100 % faster in the yard, Healer's Hands /
  Saint's Touch count as a born healer (+1 / +2 background points) and Born Leader as an
  instructor (`jobs.ts aptitude`).
- **Everywhere a hero is shown:** a chip on the hero card (✧ common, ✦ rare in gold), the
  trait's line and effects on the hero profile, the chip in the Party Board list (tooltip:
  name, rarity, line, effects), a trait filter in the Registry (every trait / rare only / one
  trait), the summon card and lineup, the promotion planner and ceremony. The effect text is
  generated from the data (`src/ui/people/traitText.ts`), so the words cannot drift.

### 2. Starting skills

A procedural 3★+ arrives with its class's signature skill (`gacha.ts startingSkillIds`). No
draw: checked against the old code over 5 seeds × 300 pulls, every id, name, grade, pity and
stream is byte-identical apart from the skill (4★+ kits already began with it). gacha.test
changed on purpose (the "empty authored skill list" test, plus a new pin of seed 98765's
first pulls).

### 3. Summon reveal 2.0 — `src/ui/summon/*`

- A ten-pull opens on **ten orbs** in each beam's opening colour (a rare pull teased a colour
  humbler, shimmering); "Skip to best" works from there. During the reveal a **strip of orbs**
  shows who is out (true colours) and who is still to come.
- **1–2★ flip fast** (0.54 s), 3★+ keep the full beat (the Normal 3★ jackpot still teases).
- The card says **who they were**: "Before the summon: hunter · Loves apple pie · Voice:
  cheerful", then **their greeting** in their voice (`speech.ts greeting`, a new bank keyed by
  Voice — "Pick me up!" — and the nine cameos' own lines).
- A **second card beat stamps** what they brought, one after another: the engraving seal, the
  Oath-weapon, each skill, the trait, the best growth grade ("S-grade STR!", A and up shout;
  lower grades read quietly) and **"Quality floor reached!"** on the pull the Rising Quality
  Floor lifted (`floorHits` mirrors the gacha's pity counting). A click before the stamps land
  stamps them at once.
- Each hero **steps out of the light as a full-body walk sprite** (sprites.ts `heroFrameUrl`,
  two frames, a few strides, then stands).
- A **canon cameo** gets a gilded double frame, "A face from the stories!" and a fanfare.
- A **pity meter** (Normal: ★★★ x/50; Advanced: ★★★★ x/30 and ★★★★★ x/90) in the corner.
- The **lineup gathers a bond group** formed by the pull under its name (`bondLabel`), and
  every mini card shows the trait (and an A/S grade shout).
- The card **scales with the viewport** (`--card-w: clamp(…)`), with phone rules (the body
  tucks against the card, the strip clears the buttons).
- **Reduced motion:** no tease in the orbs, no stepping, no stamping slam — every beat is kept,
  calmly. **Lane H's music hook** (`useMusic({ scene: 'summon', tier })`) is kept: the overview
  plays the first layer, each beam raises it, the lineup holds the best pull's.
- Pure pieces in `revealPlan.ts` (`isFastBeat`, `stampDelay`, `overviewOrbs`, `orbTier`,
  `bestGrade`, `gradeShout`, `cardStamps`, `floorHits`, `lineupGroups`); new CSS in
  `reveal2.css`.

### 4. The promotion ceremony

- **`promotionPreview(hero, seed, choice?)`** — pure, seeded exactly as `completePromotion`:
  star and cap, grades before/after (and which got the potential bonus), bases after, the
  classes and skills on offer, the default skill, the engraving (evolve from→to, or the % chance
  one awakens — the outcome stays a secret) and the trait before/after.
- **`PROMOTE_HERO` gains optional `heroClass` and `skillId`** (append-only fields). A classless
  hero reaching 3★ is offered two callings — the chamber's own pick and one more from a stream
  of its own; a hero who knows their class skill is offered three learnable skills — the
  chamber's draw first, two more from `promotion-offer`. The choices ride on `hero.promotion`
  (optional fields, no schema bump) and survive the timer and a gem skip; anything not on offer
  is refused as `Error('startPromotion: … not on offer …')`. The skill choice never moves the
  rest of the roll (grades, bases, an engraving's awakening). A pick learned some other way
  before the bell falls back to the chamber's.
- **The growth model — decided with the sim: 'potential'** (`TUNING.ceremony`): every grade +1,
  one seeded grade +1 more, within the new ceiling. See "Balance" below for why.
- **The Promotion Chamber's planner** (`src/ui/promotion/PromotionPlanner.tsx`): "⬆ Promote…"
  opens the preview and the choices (calling cards with the skill each brings; skill cards
  with role and description; the chamber's pick marked) before "⬆ Begin the promotion". Only
  what overrules the chamber rides on the command.
- **`PromotionReveal`** overlay: the chamber opens, the hero steps out, the stars burst, the
  grades tick up (+1 / +2 badges), the new calling, every new skill (the class skill and any the
  released levels unlocked), the engraving evolving or awakening, a trait awakening; a click
  shows it all, reduced motion starts there. **`PromotionCeremonyHost`** (one line in App)
  detects a completed promotion by diffing snapshots (a living hero whose star rose out of the
  chamber), queues several ("Next (2 more)", "Skip the rest"), holds while a climb is on
  screen, and leaves a catch-up after time away to Isel's letter (as the toasts do).
- **`displayStar` is cleared on promotion** (B41, already in completePromotion; kept and tested).
- **The bots choose:** the calling the top eight has fewest of, and the offered skill worth the
  most skill CP (a heal or a guard first when the top five has none) — PROMOTE_CLASS /
  PROMOTE_SKILL in the lever counts (casual 8–12 / 12–18, engaged 4–14 / 12–22 a run).

### 5. Sim & balance

The full numbers are in the dated section of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-29-balance-pass.md`.
In short: `npm run sim -- 30 3` finals casual 79/79/79 → 79/79/78, engaged 80/84/79 → 84/79/79,
whale 89×3 → 89×3; on more seeds the lane makes heroes a little sturdier and a little faster
(casual × 16: mean final F72.8 → F76.2, dead 37.8 → 30.6; engaged × 14: F80.9 → F82.5, dead
10.9 → 8.4), after trimming the trait numbers about a fifth. The combat gate (`combatMetrics
30 6`, from F41) passes before and after: AoE 19.5 → 19.2 %, rounds 3.40 → 3.60, enemies act
99.1 → 99.7 %, replay 48.0 → 51.4 s; deaths per attempt 0.167 → 0.212 on six seeds, where two
unlucky casual runs dominate — the same casual measure over 16 seeds falls 0.887 → 0.723.

Why 'potential' growth: raising a 1★ to 5★ gives about the same mean grade as the re-roll
(6.30 vs 6.19) but an S only 14 % of the time (re-roll 41 %, the same as a summoned 5★), and
over 16 casual seeds the free player does no worse (F76.1 vs F72.9). +1/+2 was tried: 71 % of
raised heroes become S-graders.

## Decisions

- **Families with a common and a rare form**, so "rarer traits at higher stars" never changes
  who a hero is: the person stays Brave; a promotion may make them a Lionheart.
- **Temperament decides the family** (not the background, which follows star and class).
- **The potential growth model** (see the balance section).
- **The preview tells the truth** except an engraving's awakening, which is a dice roll the
  chamber shows as its chance.
- **No ceremony after time away**: Isel's letter and the toasts already tell that story, and an
  overlay over the letter read badly in the browser check.
- **The bonus grade is uniform over the grades below the ceiling**, not drawn toward the
  summon's best: the shape a hero was born with is kept by the +1s, and the bonus stays a
  surprise the preview reveals.

## Left for later

  planner instead.
- Promotion's skill pool is the old innate pool: a warrior can be offered another class's
  signature skill (Arcane Burst). Lane F kept that pool for the gacha pins; a class-aware pool
  is a small follow-up.
- Traits do not yet speak in the lobby (a Brave hero's line before a floor, a Night-Fighter
  awake at night); speech.ts topics could read `traitOf`.
- A cameo can only be met in the browser through a lucky pull; a dev hook to force one would
  make QA easier.
