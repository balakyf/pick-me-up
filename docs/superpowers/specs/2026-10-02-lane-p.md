> **Status:** done · **Date:** 2026-10-04 · **Base:** `cd5287f` (waves 1–7 merged) · **Branch:** `worktree-agent-a1c61c53fe27a3db2`
> **Brief:** pillar 1 ("every death is a choice, never an ambush") and pillar 3 ("fights are short dramas you can read and steer"): missions on the filler floors, read before entering, and a teaching curve (a coach and floors that teach). Lane O (endgame) ran in parallel.

## What shipped

### 1 · The mission table (`src/engine/content/missions.ts`)

Before: a filler floor was a Subjugation, with a Survival roll in the Ruins, Survival/Escape late, a Seizure roll on the coast. Now every act with a table draws its filler mission from seven kinds built on the engine's primitives, weighted to fit the act (Layer 2 §4.4):

| Act | Weights | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I Prairie | Subjugation 5 · Survival 2 · Hunt 2 (only F7, F9 draw; the rest are teaching floors) | canon F1–9 "Subjugation / Conquest / Survival / Explore" |
| II Ruins | Subjugation 4 · Escort 3 · Survival 2 · Hunt 1 | canon F12–19 "Guard": people to get out, assassins in the halls |
| III Swamp | Subjugation 3 · Escape 3 · Survival 2 · Hunt 1 · Escort 1 | the mire sinks under the party |
| IV Coast | Seizure 4 · Subjugation 2 · Escort 1 | canon F31–35 Seizure: a treasure coast |
| V Order (F43–69) | Defense 3 · Hunt 3 · Subjugation 3 · Survival 1 · Escape 1 · Escort 1 | a siege, and the Order's officers |
| VI Inflection | Survival 3 · Hunt 2 · Defense 2 · Subjugation 2 · Escape 1 | a horde, with leaders worth hunting |

- **Kinds:** Subjugation (annihilate) · Survival (outlast the act's bell) · Escape (reach) · **Defense** (defend 2 waves, each filled to 60 % of the budget) · **Hunt** (defeat the marked leader: the wave's strongest foe, at the back, +3 levels, carrying one charged move) · **Escort** (reach 32 steps + protect a hero-side NPC: a Refugee, a Pearl Diver, an Order Deserter; enemies fill 85 % of the budget) · Seizure (acquire from a carrier).
- **Draws** are `rngFor(seed, 'filler-mission', floor)`, a new stream: the enemies keep the `'floor'` stream, so a plain Subjugation draws exactly the enemies it always did (tested).
- **Untouched:** the anchors, the F36–40 loop (its floors keep the legacy mix), the Wall (F81–89) and the floors past ninety (lane O's). `buildFillerEncounter` without a seed is the legacy builder, so the Daily Dungeon and the weekly trial are unchanged.
- **New content:** the charged `e_haymaker` ("{name} winds up a huge swing at {target}…", single target, ×1.6, cooldown 4) in `enemySkills.ts`; three escort templates and their looks (`ui/pixel/missionLooks.ts`, registered in `sprites.ts`); a 🎯 icon for Hunt.
- **Fair:** the forecast runs the real fight (`prepareFloorBattle`), so the escort, the waves and the leader are in its odds; the escort shows in its "falls in N %" line; the leader's move is in "Before the fight" ("Goblin winds up Haymaker — Guard answers it").

### 3 · The coach (`src/ui/qol/coach.ts`, `CoachTip.tsx`, `coach.css`)

A pure function from the state and the floor ahead to the next lesson. Rules: the lessons come in order (elements → lines → skills → the mission → big moves and Guard → Mark/Focus/Protect → Retreat → heals and shields → statuses → boss phases → morale); only the first unseen one can show, when the floor features it (each has a floor it falls back to, so the curve never stalls); a Master far past a lesson is not taught it (`until`); a tip is latched once per save as `coach:<id>` through `GUIDE_STEP` (as lane M's `story:` keys), on "Got it" or when the party goes in with it on the board.

- **War room:** `CoachCard` (Isel's face, the title, two or three sentences, "Got it", "Turn tips off") under the briefing.
- **Battle:** `CoachBattleTip` in `BattleScene` (a `coach` prop the tower passes) shows the pending lesson the first time its moment plays (a foe winds up, a hero is stunned/poisoned/burnt/bled, a boss turns). It cannot write the save mid-battle (a mid-battle order revises the attempt the store just recorded), so the tower latches it when the replay ends.
- **Settings:** "Isel's tips" (`coachTips`, on by default) in Settings ▸ Game; the card's "Turn tips off" sets it too.
- **First Steps** counts only `step:` keys, `talk` and `dismissed`: coach latches do not change its count (tested).
- Along a fresh climb the lessons land at F1, F2, F3, F5, F6, F8, F10, F11, F13, F20, F21 (tested).

### 4 · The filler briefing (`src/ui/tower/FillerBriefing.tsx`, `missionBrief.ts`, `missions.css`)

Every floor between the anchors now has a card where lane M's anchor briefing sits: the floor's name (or a set piece's title), the mission, the act's line for that mission, a line of flavour, **To win** (the forecast's own `missionLines`) and **Lost if** (`failLines`: "Refugee falls: the mission fails at once.", "The whole party falls before the bell."…), and on one floor in three Isel's word. A hunt's target reads "the marked Goblin" everywhere in the war room. The escort is among the free pre-battle Protects (👑 chip in "Before the fight").

### 5 · Story on the filler floors, and the F45 key (`src/engine/content/story.ts`, additive)

- `FILLER_STORY` (per act: a briefing line per mission kind, three flavour lines, two Isel lines) and `FILLER_FLOOR_STORY` (the teaching floors and F47). I kept the "event" in the briefing rather than as a battle line: `storyBeats` reads only the log, and the Daily Dungeon's logs look like filler floors.
- **The key pays off at F47.** Lane M left "it was hers" open. The lock the key turned is Priasis's vault, two floors up, held by the Order: a Seizure from the vault's keeper (`ragna_vault`). Clearing it reveals a new truth, **Her Key** (`hidden.ts`): *the Al Ragna came down from a world that ended when its Master cleared the ninetieth floor; Taonier was built by survivors*. A new Priasis beat (`vault`, after 47) carries her last letter ("If my key found you, then I am gone… We came down from a world that ended, Master. Do not let yours.") and Isel's lobby line. It ties her two suns and the golden crown to the F90 secret. There are now 11 truths (7 still needed to subvert).

### 6 · The bots (`src/sim/sim.ts`, `missionSense.ts`)

- New profile flag `missions` (engaged, whale): before a floor the bot reads the mission (`missionDirective`): the free MARK on the foe whose fall wins it (a leader, a carrier, a boss; never a looming giant) and the free PROTECT on the escort. Its forecast reads the same plan. Lever: `MISSION_READ`.
- `orderAnswers` now tries a standing Guard (`guard onTelegraph` at tick 1) first when a big move hurt the party, before the reactive Guard and the Unleash.
- The casual bot does neither (it never orders).

## Decisions

- **The loop and the Wall keep their logic.** The brief said so, and F80+ is lane O's.
- **Gentle means a lighter fill**, not weaker enemies: the same pool, 85 % of the budget.
- **Leaders hang back.** A hunt's leader stands in the back line, so the mark (free) is what wins the floor quickly; it also gives F6's goblin time to wind up its swing (at the front it died before it acted).
- **Latch on Enter.** A tip on the board when the party goes in counts as read; otherwise a Master who never clicks "Got it" would block the curriculum on one lesson.
- **A truth for the key** makes the vault matter to the F90 choice, which is what the key was always pointing at.
