## What shipped

### 1 · The Siege of the Wailing Wall (F80)

Canon: the Fragment Series for every Master, Pryos Al Ragna as the boss, and "nearly all of Han's forces excluding the main party died there". F80 (`content/anchors.ts`) is now a **siege in four stages**, still the canon Conquest:

| Stage | Wave | What comes out |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · the approach | 4 shards, 1 knight | the Wall empties itself at the ram |
| 2 · the gate | 2 knights, a warden, a shard | its knights come out to meet the ram |
| 3 · the breach | 2 wardens, 2 knights | they hold the hole |
| 4 · Pryos | Pryos Al Ragna (+40, his two seal phases) | his title card and entrance |

- **Objectives:** hold off all four waves (`defend 4`), **keep the Siege Ram alive** (`protect siege_ram`: a new ally object, iron-shod, sturdy, at the floor's level +40), and defeat Pryos. If the ram breaks, the siege fails.
- **Budget:** the Wall's own (`wallPowerMult` 2.6 × `anchorBudgetMult`) × `ENDGAME.siege.budgetMult` **1.45**, and the siege stands on **exactly** that budget (`buildScaledAnchor` trims the 8 % elite steps with `scaleWaves`, F80 only). Fifteen foes instead of ten spread the CP thinner, so the multiplier buys back the Wall's weight; four fights in one battle also make the Wall less of a lottery.
- **On screen:** the objective HUD (Wave n/4, the ram's HP bar), Isel's opening line ("The siege begins. Keep the ram moving…"), a narrated line as stages two and three come on (`ANCHOR_STORY[80].waves`, lane M's `storyBeats` gains a `wave` kind with its `STORY_MS`), then Pryos's own card and entrance. The ram and the F86 banner are pixel objects (`ui/ending/siegeArt.ts`, registered in `sprites.ts` `ALLY_OBJECTS`).
- Deterministic and the same for every Master (`endgame.test.ts`: two accounts, one Wall; one attempt replays bit-identically).

|---|---|---|

| F82 | The Shard Archive | Seizure: the archive's keeper (the strongest foe) |
| F83 | The Hollow Garrison | Survival (900 ticks, two waves) |

### 3 · The F90 decision and its consequences

The decision is still `ATTEMPT_FLOOR` (with or without `subvert`). What it now leaves behind, for the rest of the world (`endgame/endgame.ts`, read through `fateOf(state)`):

| | The world ended (a plain clear) | The world was spared (Subvert) |
|---|---|---|
| F91–100 foes | **sated**: every foe at ×0.85 (`scaleWaves`) | as written |
| F91–100 gold | ×0.5 ("no one left below to pay") | as written |
| once | — | **tribute**: 300 💎 and 40,000 ◆ |
| the summit | Tell's last draft as written | Tell **cannot call back the Herald** you refused (`echo_herald` leaves her reserves) |
| the lobby | the world canvas greys | a warm tint |
| Isel / Priasis | the `ended` beats, and past the summit "her pen is still on the floor" | the `saved` beats, and "I sent Priasis's people the names" |
| Act VIII's card | comes back once on F91 with a mournful variant (`ACT_STORY_AFTER`) | the spared variant |

So the choice has a price both ways: ending the world makes the last ten floors easier and poorer; sparing it keeps them hard and pays once. The war room frames both before the choice (`ChoiceStakes` under the Herald's briefing) and lists what changed after it (with the epilogue to replay).

- **Persisted:** `GameState.endgame?` (optional; `endgameOf` defaults it — **no schema bump**). `sealFate` records `{ kind, day, truths }` on the attempt that cleared F90 (`playFloor` §6d) and pays the tribute. `tower.worldEnded/worldSaved` stay the source of truth for `fateOf`.
- **Tests:** the fate survives a save/load; the sated floors and the half gold; the tribute and the missing echo; `reduce` ≡ `attemptFloorWithResult` and bit-identical replays.

### 4 · Memories of the Tower (anchor replays)

Named apart from the weekly **Echo Trial** (lane A): the trial is a gauntlet of echoes; a memory is one floor, remembered. Command **`RELIVE_FLOOR { floor, difficulty, heroIds }`** (`endgame/relive.ts`, `reliveWithResult` in the store):

- Floors: every anchor and every floor that hid a truth, below the world's end (F89 included, F90 is "a wound, not a memory"). **Refused** for an uncleared floor (`relive: F20 has not been cleared yet.`), a floor with no memory, an empty or duplicated team, someone not at home, or no memories left.
- Difficulty: **Faded** (foes ×0.8, 10 % of the floor's gold, *no truths*), **As it was** (×1, 20 %, truths), **Vivid** (×1.3, 40 %, truths).
- **Nobody dies** (pristine copies at full Sanity; the replay is non-lethal), but each hero pays 8 Sanity. Three a world-week.
- A missed truth found again pays **half** its first reward, counts toward Subverting F90 (`tower.hiddenFound`), and is listed in `endgame.recovered`. Lane C's truth rule now counts recoverable truths (`truthStanding.reachable`; its test changed on purpose) and the Chronicle says "relive the floor to find it".
- UI: `ui/ending/Memories.tsx` beside Raids and the Echo Trial (the three windows now open in a portal: inside the sticky command panel the floor list painted over them — found in the browser).

### 5 · The epilogue and the credits

`ui/ending/Ending.tsx` + `EndingHost.tsx` (mounted once in App, opened through `endingBus`):

- **When:** after the F90 results (TowerScreen's `resultDone`), or by itself in the lobby when a sealed fate's epilogue was never seen (a reload), waiting for any open window (Isel's letter). Latched once per save as `story:epilogue:<fate>` (lane M's latch). **Replay** from the Menu ("✦ Epilogue") or the war room's fate box.
- **Stills:** five act backdrops (`drawBattleBg` via lane M's `actBackdropUrl`), one line each (`EPILOGUE[fate]`), grey for an ended world; then **Isel's last word**.
- **Credits:** every one of the fallen — name, star and class, where and when they fell (or "given to the Synthesis", "lost to a rival's captors"), their **last words** (`lastWordsTogether`: no two share them) and **Isel's eulogy** (`iselFor`/`eulogy`) — then the **legends** of earlier worlds, **those who came home**, and the numbers (cycle, fate, the day it was decided, highest floor, heroes called, fallen, survivors, truths, Master Level). `creditsOf` (engine) lists exactly the Memorial's graves and exactly the living.
- **Skippable** at every step (Next, Skip to the credits, Skip the credits, ✕/Esc). **Reduced motion:** no pan, no roll — the credits become a list to read with a Next button.

### 6 · The New Cycle

Command **`NEW_CYCLE`** (`endgame/cycle.ts`), refused until the fate is sealed. It builds a fresh world from `createAccount(hash(seed, 'cycle', n))` and carries over **exactly**:

- the Master: level and XP, minigame skills, login streak, the simulated wallet and monthly pass, and the **gems** in hand;
- the **Enemy Codex**;
- the onboarding steps already learned (`step:` keys; the free tutorial pull comes again, the new world's story is new);
- guild membership and PvP rating;
- the **legends**: up to 12 of the world's fallen (statues first, then the deepest), kept across cycles (60 at most);
- the **history** (`CycleRecord` per finished world) and the **cycle counter**.

The new world's tower is harder: `worldMultFor` × `cycleMult` = 1 + 0.12 × cycle (levels, budget and gold). `reduce` handles it before the life/estate reactions (the old roster's reactions don't apply to a new world). The Ending's last page asks first (what you keep, what you leave behind), then a "Cycle II · A new world" card opens on the fresh lobby.

## Decisions

- **The endgame slice is optional** (`endgame?` + `endgameOf`), like `challengeOf`; old saves load unchanged and read as the first world. No schema bump, no migration.
- **Presentation latches use lane M's story latch**; only state that matters to the rules went into commands (`NEW_CYCLE`, `RELIVE_FLOOR`).
- **The siege keeps the canon mission type** (Conquest) and becomes a siege through its objectives, stages and lines, so the anchor's story checks and icons need nothing new.
- **The F81–89 missions reshape the budgeted fill rather than authoring units**, so the post-Wall budget stays the balance lane's knob; only the gentle ramp is new.
- **Fate's effects are post-hoc scaling** (`scaleFoe`: HP, attack, defence; speed and crit stay), so a fight's rhythm and the RNG streams are untouched.
- **Reliving is a memory, not a raid:** it can never kill, so its price is Sanity and a weekly cap, and truths need the true or vivid memory.
- **Day numbers** in the credits count from the Master's first day (graves keep the absolute world-day).

## What is left

- **The Memorial** (lane L's panel) does not show the legends yet; they live in `endgame.legends` and roll in the credits. A "Legends" shelf in the Memorial is a small read of `endgameOf(state).legends`.
- **Bots never end the world**: they subvert whenever they know seven truths, and Reliving now gets most of them there. A bot that prefers the sated, cheaper climb (or a profile flag) would exercise the ended branch in the sim; today only tests and the browser do.
- **The Herald** holds two of three whales for 30 days at 60 days, while engaged parties that arrive later clear it in one or two attempts (lane F's `heraldPowerMult`): lane R should look at F90 with Reliving's truths in view (Subvert strips the aegis).
- **Reliving's loop:** a bot (and a Master) can relive the same floor every day when its truth is out of reach (a whale relived 39 times for one truth). A hint of which difficulty or party would do it, or a cooldown per floor, would help.
- **New Cycle depth:** the new world reuses the same story (Priasis, the same anchors) and only `cycleMult` changes the tower; legends could come back as cameos, and the summit's Tell could remember the last cycle.
- **The tribute** is not shown on the results card (the war room's fate box lists it); lane K's results could add one line.
- The war room's sticky Enter panel covers the F90 stakes box on a phone until the Master scrolls (the same as lane M's briefing).
- Raw refusals from the engine with numbers in them (`F20 has not been cleared yet.`) are not translated (the list only offers cleared floors, so they rarely show).
