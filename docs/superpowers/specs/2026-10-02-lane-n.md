# Lane N · Hero sheet, picker & gear

> **Status:** Done · **Date:** 2026-10-04 · **Base:** `530a3f2` (waves 1–6 merged) · **Worktree branch:** `worktree-agent-a5eb0bbc2a355488f`
> **Plan:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-02-dream-game.md` (pillar 2: heroes are people, not stat blocks — "one hero sheet").

A hero now has one window. The Registry, the Party Board, the lobby's tracker and profile links, every facility picker and the Armory all open the same tabbed hero sheet. Wherever the Master picks a hero, one picker does it, with the same search, sorts, filters and names. Gear has a real flow: an inventory grid, side-by-side compare with stat deltas, one-click "equip best" (a new command the bots use), forge orders that say what the smiths are doing, and gear left by the dead marked with who carried it. Promotion no longer offers another class's signature skill.

## What shipped

### 1. The hero sheet: `src/ui/hero/HeroSheet.tsx`
- **Header** (on every tab): bust (framed in the star colour), shown stars, level/cap, true CP, class with born trade, element, favor, morale pips with the band, and the status line. "Find on the map" appears in the lobby. "Promote to N★…" appears when the hero can promote and opens the planner.
- **Overview:** the trait (full), the engraving and its blurb, the bond group, morale with every reason (lane L's `MoraleBreakdown`), what they are saying, estate notes, mood gauges and grief, friends (bond chips and floors together) and the bond group list, the job (with the job picker in a fold), and favor and gifts (`HeroBond`, in a fold).
- **Stats:** level and XP bar (or held XP at the cap); true CP and how much of it comes from gear; growth grades with base attributes; every combat stat with gear's share.
- **Skills:** each skill with grade, level/max, XP bar, role and blurb, passive or bound; the skill they practise in free time; merges they are working toward (inputs with levels, "merges on its own when both reach Lv N"), and the Transfer Station's early fuse and evolve options with cost and reason.
- **Gear:** the GearPanel (see §3). For the fallen: what they carried at the end, and who carries it now.
- **Story:** their life before the summon (trade, voice, hours, food, hobby; "A face from the stories" for cameos), arrival day, temperament gauges, memories, and their chronicle. For the fallen, the grave comes first: the cause, the day, days served, last words and mourners.
- Tabs are a real tablist: `aria-selected`, left/right arrow keys, and one panel. It is a `PixelWindow` (`role=dialog`), so hotkeys stand aside. On desktop the panel has a minimum height so the centred window doesn't jump between tabs.
- **One sheet, opened from anywhere** (`hero/sheetBus.ts`, `HeroSheetHost.tsx`, mounted once in `App`): `openHeroSheet(id, tab?)` and `openPromotionPlanner(id)`. The lobby keeps its own sheet state (lane L's `setProfile` call sites are unchanged) but renders `HeroSheet`. It also registers its camera, so a sheet opened from a facility picker can still "find" the hero. The old `HeroProfile` is gone from `lifeWindows.tsx`; its pieces (`memoryLine`, `JobPicker`, the temperament list, now exported as `PERSONALITY_TRAITS`) are reused, not forked.
- **The Registry** opens the sheet on a card click; the expanded card and the separate bond panel are folded into the sheet. **The Party Board** has an ⓘ on every list row, and each picker row has one too.

### 2. The shared picker: `src/ui/hero/HeroPicker.tsx` + `heroPicker.ts`
- Search (accent- and case-folded); sorts by CP (true CP), stars (shown stars, so the whale-bait lie holds), level, morale (lane L's score, the fallen last), class, name and element, with a flip; filters for class, element, "available only" and, where it fits, living/fallen. Ties fall back to CP, then id, so the order is stable.
- **Naming** follows lane A's rule (`pickerName` + `HeroTag`): first name unless another living hero shares it, always with star and level.
- **Each place keeps its own validation.** A `refusal(hero)` gives the reason a place can't take a hero; the row is greyed out with the reason, and "available only" hides it. The engine still refuses on its own. The reasons live in `hero/refusals.ts`: away (fallen, captive, Ruins, bounty), promoting, drilling, not at the cap, highest star, materials, survivor ≠ sacrifice.
- **Used in:**
  - Promotion Chamber: who is waiting at the cap, then the planner.
  - Synthesis: the survivor, and the sacrifices (weakest first).
  - Transfer: donor, recipient and fuser; a hero can't be both donor and recipient.
  - Training: the new drill.
  - Kitchen: "who needs the kitchen", lowest morale first (browse).
  - Tactical Center: the party on duty (browse).
  - Armory: the hero to outfit.
  - Party Board: the new **swap**. Click ⇄ on a filled slot, or click an empty slot, to choose who stands there. The current occupant can be taken off the board, and heroes already on the board show "in slot N". The hero status hint is the refusal.
- `partyBoard.boardHeroes` now delegates to the picker's `pickHeroes`, so the board's list and the picker can't drift (its 9 tests pass unchanged).

### 3. Gear
- **Engine — `src/engine/equipment/loadout.ts`** (pure, no RNG, ties by id):
  - `itemScore`: an item's worth is the Combat Power of its stat block (legacy EVA/ACC folded in), so "best" agrees with true CP. The multipliers applied later (passives, engraving, favor, morale) scale every item alike.
  - `itemDelta(from, to)`: the compare (only the stats that move, with from/to/delta, plus the CP change).
  - `bestLoadout` / `equipBest`: per slot, the best item the hero can wield, used only if it is strictly worth more than what they wear. It never takes from another living hero and never picks a weapon whose element differs from the hero's (that is the Master's call). On a tie the hero's own Oath-Blade wins, then what they already wear.
  - `heirloomOf`: the latest grave that carried an item.
- **Command `EQUIP_BEST { heroId }`** (appended to the union; `reduceCore` case). Refusals: `equipBest: unknown hero …`, `… is not alive`, `… nothing better to equip for …`.
- **The bots** (`sim.ts`): the top five use `EQUIP_BEST` (when `bestLoadout` finds something) instead of the bots' own grade loop. It is a counted lever.
- **GearPanel** (`hero/GearPanel.tsx`, the sheet's Gear tab and the Armory's loadout): three slots with the worn item, its stats and tags, and Unequip. "Compare" lists every other item of that slot, best first, with stat deltas and CP change. It marks items another hero wears (not equipable), Oath-weapons bound elsewhere, and blades that would change the wielder's element. The "Equip best" button says what it will change before you press it.
- **The Armory** (`facilities/Armory.tsx`):
  - The forge buttons.
  - "Equip best for the party".
  - The **inventory grid**: filters for slot, grade (with counts) and worn/free/left by the fallen. Each cell shows grade, stats, who wears it, the bond 🔗 and "✝ carried by …".
  - **Side-by-side compare**: pick an item, then a hero, to see what they wear beside it, with the deltas and Equip.
  - "Outfit a hero" (the picker, then the GearPanel, noting heroes with "better gear free").
  - The anvil, now in a fold.
- **Forge orders shown clearly** (`facilities/Forge.tsx`, replacing lane L's `ForgeOrderSection` in the Armory's panel): the order buttons, what the standing order means, and the forge's state in one line:
  - cold
  - no smith
  - hammering a grade/slot at N%, with a progress bar
  - the party fully equipped
  - stalled for want of gold or stones (in warning colour)
  - the next piece

  It also lists the smiths by name, the per-piece cost, and the last four pieces forged (chronicle lines).
- `facilities/gearText.ts`: item names (masterworks such as "Bram's B Plate" now translate too), stat labels and signed deltas. `Armory.tsx` re-exports `itemLabel` for the summon reveal.

### 4. Wave-5 follow-ups

   - The class's signature comes first, as before.
   - After it, the pool never holds another class's signature. It holds any learnable skill that is no class's signature, then the trained skills anyone can learn, excluding merge recipes (inputs and results stay the Training Center's) and the skills levels unlock on their own (a class's kit comes with its levels).
   - Classless heroes draw from the same shared pool.
   - Today the post-signature pool is Pain Tolerance, Insight, Flame Resistance and Indomitability (enough for the ceremony's three offers).
   - Seeded promotion draws change **on purpose** (stream order unchanged). The `gacha.test.ts` pins are untouched; the summon's innate pool is the gacha's own.
2. **The advisor's "Promote…"** opens the promotion planner in its own window (`PromotionWindow` in `HeroSheetHost.tsx`) instead of dispatching `PROMOTE_HERO` with no choice. The hero sheet's Promote button opens the same window.
3. **Phones (390 wide): the Party Board list keeps the trait chip.** The `display: none` rule in `people.css` is gone; the name row wraps the chip under the name. Filled slots got room under the pips for the ⇄ button.
4. **Dev-only cameo reveal** (`hero/DevCameo.tsx`): in a dev build or with `?dev=1` (lane H's `devToolsEnabled`, never in normal play), the summon chamber shows "Debug · cameo reveal (name)". ## Decisions

- **Item worth is item CP.** It is monotone in every stat and agrees with true CP. A role-weighted score (a mage valuing M.ATK) would disagree with the CP the Master sees. Weapons give P.ATK and M.ATK alike today anyway.
- **Equip best never strips another hero**, and never changes a hero's element by itself. Both are visible choices in compare.
- **The class-aware pool leaves out kit skills** (they unlock with levels: offering them would waste the pick and show twice in the planner) and merge inputs (lane F marked them `learnable: false` on purpose).
- **One sheet, two hosts.** The lobby renders its own `HeroSheet` so lane L's eight `setProfile` call sites stay untouched. Everything else goes through the bus host in `App`. The bus host sits above every scene, including facility windows.
- **Kitchen and Tactical** have no hero commands, so their pickers browse: a click opens the sheet. The job assignment selects (`StaffSection` in `ui/life`) stay as they were.
- **The Registry's trait filter stays.** The Registry is a card gallery, and the picker is for choosing.

## What is left

- **The advisor's 'equip' tip** still dispatches a single `EQUIP_ITEM`. It could become `EQUIP_BEST`, or open the hero sheet on the Gear tab.
- **Job assignment** (`StaffSection` in `ui/life/lifePanels.tsx`) still uses a native select. Moving it to the picker would need lane L's area. `ForgeOrderSection` there is now unused and can go.
- **The gacha's innate pool** (`gacha.ts`, a summon's random learnable skill) is still class-blind: a 1★ farmer can arrive with Arcane Burst. Making it class-aware moves the `gacha.test.ts` pins, so it was left on purpose for a lane that is ready to re-pin.
- **Balance (lane R):** with guards instead of off-class nukes, Wall fights stall more often (fewer deaths, more attempts at F80; engaged seed 1 no longer breaks it). If the Wall should break as often as before, look at the post-signature pool's power, or add class-flavoured offensive skills to it.
- **Pickers in other lanes' places** (the raid planner and the weekly trial in `ui/challenge`, the bounty board in the estate) still use their own chips. They can adopt `HeroPicker` with a `refusal`.
- **Sheet extras:** a per-viewer remembered tab, a "next/previous hero" stepper, and a gear tab for comparing two heroes.
- Pre-existing missing French found on the way: the place blurbs for the dormitory, tavern, infirmary, garden, memorial, library, watchtower and market; "The bar is empty. For now."; "No one is visiting.".
