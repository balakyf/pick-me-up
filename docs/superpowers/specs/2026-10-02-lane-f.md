# Lane F · Skills & roles

> **Status:** Done · **Date:** 2026-10-02 · **Base:** 8cf6073 · **Worktree branch:** `worktree-wf_b5387452-646-2`
> **Plan:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-02-dream-game.md` (pillar 3: fights are short dramas you can read and steer; opportunity O14).

Five classes now fight five ways. A warrior raises his shield and roars so the foes must face
him; a spearman runs a thrust through two foes and cracks their armour; a thief's flurry is
three cuts that may open a bleed; an archer marks the boss so everyone hits it harder; a mage
burns, binds, wards and mends. Anyone can learn to bind a wound at the Training Center. A
priest heals her knight, a shaman spits poison, a knight taunts, Halgiraf breathes fire. The
replay shows it: icons over every head and in the party rows, words that pop as a status takes
hold, DoT and shield numbers, captions for every beat.

## What shipped (brief item by item)

### 1. The effects model (engine)

- `SkillDef.effects?: SkillEffectDef[]` and `hits?: number` (types.ts). Effect kinds: `heal`
  (from the caster's mAtk or the recipient's max HP), `regen` (heal over time), `shield` (an
  absorb pool from mAtk, max HP or pDef), `buff` / `debuff` of a stat (`atk`, `def`, `spd`,
  `crit` in points, `guard` = damage taken; a guard *debuff* is a Mark), `dot` (bleed / poison
  / burn, or `'element'`: fire and light burn, earth, water and dark poison, the rest bleed),
  `stun` (pushes the action gauge back by a % of a turn), `taunt`, `sp` (restore or drain).
  Each effect may say `to: 'self' | 'allies' | 'ally-lowest' | 'targets'` (default: the
  skill's own targets). Magnitudes are integer percents that grow by `perLevel`; a stun's
  chance grows instead. `skills.ts effectAt` resolves them; `resolveSkillEffect` carries
  `effects` and `hits` into the SkillEffect the sim reads. Every skill id is unchanged.
- New `SkillTarget`s: `self`, `ally-lowest`, `ally-threatened` (the friend the foes are lined
  up on — a shield's mark; one more than the brief named, because "shield the most threatened
  ally" needs it), `all-allies`, `front-row` (the front-most line, with lane D's falloff) and
  `cleave` (a foe and its nearest neighbour at `cleavePct` 50 %).
- **Durations are in the caster's own turns**, turned into ticks at the cast from its speed
  (`turns × 1000 / spd`, at least `minStatusTicks`). A tick-based clock alone would make a
  3-tick taunt nothing for a slow knight and an eternity for a fast thief.
- `MutUnit.statuses` (one per key; a re-cast refreshes: the longer clock and the stronger
  value win) tick in the main loop **before** gauges fill: timed statuses count down, DoTs and
  regeneration pulse once per caster-turn, and a DoT can kill (waves, shields and the mission
  are re-checked then). A shield soaks blows **and** DoT pulses (Indomitability "slows
  bleed/poison", as canon has it); a blow it swallows whole lands as no 'hit' at all.
- Buffs bend attack, defence and speed as `× (100 + pct) / 100`, exactly the snapshot when
  there are none; crit adds points to the same crit roll (the draw count never changes).
- **Multi-hit**: each hit rolls crit and variance and logs its own 'hit'; riders apply once
  per struck unit; a follow-up comes once after the last hit.
- **RNG gating**: a status roll is drawn only when `0 < chance < 100` after the target's
  statusRes shaves it (`chance × 100 / (100 + statusRes)`). Allies always accept. Heals,
  shields, buffs, taunts and sure statuses draw nothing, so the golden battle and every
  battle without chance-based statuses replay bit-identically.
- **The SP rhythm** (`TUNING.roles`): +`spPerAction` 4 SP after each action, and
  +`spPerBarTaken` 12 SP per whole bar of max HP lost (banked in integers, so small cuts add
  up). A support cast's 'act' carries `spAfter`, as every act now does (for lane E's SP bars).
- **Heal fatigue and weariness**: each skill heal a unit has already taken closes 25 % less
  (floor 25 %), and every heal and shield holds 25 % less per 100 ticks the fight has run.
  Clutch heals stay big; no healer out-heals a whole wave forever, and a long siege grinds to a
  decision.
- **Stalemate guard**: if the party's own actions stop wearing the foes on the field down (a
  new low at least 3 % of their max HP) for 6 actions per living hero (at least 12), the party
  falls back with lane D's 'futile' beat. Never on a Survival or an Escape. Futility itself
  now knows SP comes back (a blow the pool can ever pay for still counts), and a
  "saving up" rule lets a hero swing for free while it saves for the one blow that can hurt
  (a mage's burst at the physical-immune Wardens) unless what it chose tends a wound.
- A support cast stays in place: its 'act' names the caster (the heals and statuses that
  follow name whom it tends), so the existing choreography never runs a healer at a friend.
  Riders never fire after the blow that ends the fight. A war cry passes mission NPCs by.

### 2. Events and presentation

  `shield` (absorbed, left), `sp`. Skill heals use the existing `heal` kind with `sourceId`

### 3. The brain (chooseSkill)

Lane D's expected-damage scoring is untouched for blows. Every effect is priced in the same
coin (HP), integer per-mille, memoised once per decision (`sightFor`):

- **heal** — HP restored to an ally below `healBelowPct` 65 % (overheal is waste, fatigue
  counted), ×`healWeightPm` 1.3, ×2 if the blows lined up on them would fell them;
  **regeneration** — the same over its pulses, below 75 %, and never on a friend about to fall
  (it would come too late).
- **shield** — what the pool would soak of the blows lined up on the recipient over its turns,
  less any shield already up; `ally-threatened` puts it on the friend the foes aim at.
- **taunt** — for every foe aiming at a friend with less life than the taunter, its blow at
  the taunter × the share of life it spares the friend; a taunter's guard also counts the
  blows its roar will draw. Not while the Master has ordered the taunter protected.
- **buff** — the ally's plain blow × % × its turns (crit: × the crit bonus; def/guard: the
  blows lined up on it), capped at that share of the HP the foes still have (unspawned waves
  included) — so it pays before a big wave, not on the last goblin; **never a buff that is
  already up** (more than half its clock left).
- **debuff** — on a foe: the damage it takes away (attack down) or the party's blows it
  sharpens (a Mark / armour break), × its odds. A skill whose point is a Mark picks **the
  toughest foe** (the boss) unless a focus order says otherwise.
- **DoT** — the pulses, capped at what the blow leaves, × odds; a self-bleed (Berserk) is a
  price at half weight, and a self-draining skill is never started below 50 % HP.
- **stun**, **SP** — the delayed blow; SP given to an ally who can use it, drained from a
  caster.
- **Kills**: a blow expected to kill also scores the victim's next blow (`killWeightPm`), so a
  healer weighs binding a wound against ending the one who keeps opening it (this cut Act V
  deaths by a third in the engaged runs).

## Decisions

- **Turns, not ticks**, for durations: a status means the same number of its caster's turns
  at F5 and at F75.
- **One status per key**, refreshed by the stronger value and longer clock: icons stay
  readable, no stacking exploits, and "never stack a buff" is a rule of the world, not only
  of the brain.
- **The Master's focus beats a foe's taunt** (orders that work, pillar 1); a taunt beats the
  class rule and the Protect order's steering.
- **Heal fatigue, weariness and the stalemate guard** instead of weaker heals: First Aid
  still saves a hero in the moment, but a whole party of sustain cannot hold the Wall for four
  minutes of replay.
- **Kill value** for blows (a lane D change, made deliberately): without it, healers healed
  while the Demon's Marksman kept killing (engaged Act V deaths 3 → 15 before, back to ~11).
- **Enemy skills live in `SKILLS`** (`enemy: true`, bound): the replay's skill names, the
  choreography's elements and the metrics' sweep detection read them with no new lookup.
- **Budgets**: the Wall 2.3 → 2.6, a one-time ×1.5 step for every floor past it (F81+), and a
  ×1.25 step for the anchors from the Herald up (a party of five Lv150 gods must still reach
  the summit; at ×1.5 they could not). Act I–VI budgets are unchanged.
