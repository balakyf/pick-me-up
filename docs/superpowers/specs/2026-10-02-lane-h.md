## 1. The audio engine (`src/ui/audio/`)

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `sequencer.ts` | Pure timing math (step time with swing, next bar / beat, the bar-quantised switch time, the look-ahead window `stepsDue`), pattern compilation (`compilePattern`: notes, chords, holds, rests, accents, ghost notes, drum letters, per-channel rate), `TrackVoice` (one track on the audio clock, intensity-gated layers, one-shots end) and `Sequencer` (wakes every 30 ms, schedules 180 ms ahead on `AudioContext.currentTime`). The timer only decides *when to look*; every note lands at an exact audio-clock time, so a busy main thread no longer jitters the music. |
| `mixer.ts` | The graph: track gains → music bus → **duck** → music volume → master → limiter; the death motif goes to the music volume past the duck; sfx bus and ambience bus → sfx volume → master; one convolver reverb per bus. Levels come from Settings (`sliderGain` is a 1.7 power curve, mute zeroes the master). The context is created only in a gesture (the first click or key press); it is **suspended on `visibilitychange` hidden** and resumed when the tab is visible again or the window gets focus. `duckMusic` / `releaseDuck`. |
| `voices.ts` | The synth: 12.5 % / 25 % pulse waves (PeriodicWave), triangle, saw, two-operator FM (e-piano, bell, brass, choir, croaky bass), unison detune, vibrato, glide, low-pass, pan and reverb send, an ADSR; a noise kit (white, 8-bit "crunch", NES short-mode LFSR "metal") with kick, snare, hats, crash, toms and clap in three styles (punchy, nes, soft brushes). |
| `tracks/` | 19 tracks composed in code (`compose.ts` has chord symbols and bass/arp/pad generators; melodies are written by hand). See §2. |
| `music.ts` | `chooseTrack(request)` (pure, tested) and the player: same track → only its intensity follows; another track → the old one stops on its **next bar line** (next beat when the bar is more than 1.6 s away, or for a boss/stinger entry) with a short fade across the seam, the new one starts on that line. `playStinger` (the death motif). |
| `sound.ts` | The public face: `sfx(name, opts)`, `playMusic(request)`, `wantedTrack()`, mute (now a Settings field), `unlockAudio()`. |
| `useSound.ts` | The scene stack from lane A, now with requests (`{ scene, night, rain, floor, boss, tier }`) and entries that retarget in place (`pushMusicEntry`, `useMusic`), so a battle can turn into its jingle and the summon's tier can rise without reordering the stack. |
| `cues.ts` | 46 synthesised cues (§3). |
| `battleSfx.ts` | Pure mapping beat → cues (tested) + `CueLimiter`. |
| `useBattleAudio.ts` | One hook BattleScene mounts: floor music, the jingle at the end, every beat's cues, the death duck + motif. |
| `ambience.ts`, `useLobbyAudio.ts` | The pure ambience mix (tested) and the live beds; `useCampMusic` (the App's base scene: title → lobby day / night / rain). |
| `uiSfx.ts` | Which cue a pressed button makes; reward toasts jingle. |
| `devAudio.ts` | `window.__pmuAudio.state()` / `.cue(name, opts)` in dev builds or with `?dev=1` (context state, wanted vs playing track, layers, bus levels, live gains, duck, ambience mix). |

**B12 (both halves) is fixed at the root:** nothing chooses music on a click (App's click handler only unlocks the context and plays the button's cue); the scene on screen chooses it. Mute keeps the wish; unmuting restarts the scene's track (checked in the browser: muted → no track, master 0; unmuted → `lobby-night` scheduled, master back).

## 2. The soundtrack

All chiptune: pulse leads with delayed vibrato, arpeggiated chords, triangle/saw/FM basses, noise drums. Every track is whole bars, loops seamlessly, and was rendered offline in Chromium through the real voices to check it is audible and never clips (6 s each at the default levels: −26.6 to −32.6 dBFS RMS, peaks ≤ 0.23; per-track `gain` evens them out).

## 3. Sound effects

`battleSfx.cuesForBeat(beat, {byId, element, nonLethal})` — tested against every event kind:

- **hit** in the beat's element (fire crackle, water splash, wind whoosh, earth thud, light ring, dark growl, steel clank), ±30 cents of stable detune per event, panned toward the side struck; **crit** (once per beat); **WEAK** chirp / **RESIST** thud accents; **IMMUNE** replaces the blow with a metallic tink.
- **cast** charge-up in the skill's element (not for basic attacks or a caster foe's basic spell); **miss**, **guard**, **heal** (once per beat), **regen**, **shield** applied / absorbing / **shattering**, statuses with their own voices (**poison** bubbles, **bleed** slice, **burn** crackle, **stun** tweeting birds, **taunt** horn, buff up / debuff down), DoT ticks (once per beat), a war cry over the party sounds once (`nth`).
- **death** (foe) / **hero-death** (hero or escort; a trial's dropout is a plain one), **cover** and **follow-up** stingers, **rivalry**, **order**, **panic**, **hp-cost** heartbeat, **battle-start**, **wave-start**, **wave-clear**, mission codes (`defeated`/`taken`/`horde-spent` good, `escort-low`/`futile` bad, `hold`/`deadline`/`escape` ticks, `shield-down`, `wakes`), **floor-mods**.
- Lane G's coming kinds already sound: `telegraph` (two-beep alarm) and `phase` (a roar), read from the kind string so no type change is needed; unknown kinds are silent.
- **Rate limits:** a sweep sounds at most 4 blows (staggered with `popupDelay`, tapering), and `CueLimiter` caps each family across beats (6 blows / 320 ms, …) — a test replays 40 eight-target sweeps at 4× and checks the cap.
- Menus: **click**, **confirm** (primary / gold buttons), **cancel** (window close), **toggle** (switches, radios), `data-sfx` overrides; **coins** when a toast announces gold or gems, a chime for other good news. Summon: charge, surge, flip, rare, legend, levelup (unchanged call sites, new voices).

## 4. Lobby ambience

`useLobbyAudio(state)` returns a `feed` LobbyWorld calls once per frame with the camera (two lines in LobbyWorld; the mix is recomputed 4× a second). Beds: rain (hiss, low-passed when the Master is inside a building), wind (band-passed noise on a slow LFO), the hearth (rumble + crackles, positional, kitchen and the tavern's estate hearth), the forge (hammer on anvil, positional, only once the Forge is built), crickets on fair nights out of winter, thunder in a storm. It steps out while a replayed battle is over the lobby and when muted. Checked in the browser: inside the Kitchen the hearth reads gain 0.25 panned left; in the garden the fog's wind rises and the walls stop muffling. (The bot saves never build the Forge, so the clang was checked by the unit test of the mix.)

## 5. Settings (`src/ui/qol/settings.ts`, `Settings.tsx`)

Stored under `localStorage 'pmu.settings'`, every read guarded and sanitised field by field (garbage, out-of-range, a corrupt entry and a throwing storage are all tested); the old `pmu.muted` carries over once. Fields: master / music / sfx volume, mute, screen shake, flashes, default battle speed (1×/2×/4×), text speed (slow / normal / fast / instant — DialogBox and a live preview line in the window), UI scale (85–130 %), reduced motion (auto / on / off, with the device's current answer shown), and the language (with pixel flags). Reachable from the Game Menu (**Settings O**), the **O** hotkey anywhere outside battles and windows, listed in the keyboard help and the lobby's help line. Changes apply at once; "Reset to defaults" leaves the language alone.

- **UI scale** zooms windows, the HUD, the scene bar, the battle's controls, toasts and the dialogue text (CSS `zoom` under `<html data-ui-scale>`); the pixel world keeps its integer fit. No horizontal scroll at 390 px even at 130 %.
- **Reduced motion:** `src/ui/motion.ts` replaces the three OS-only detectors (BattleScene/stageFx, the summon reveal, the estate/sky layer — and a fourth in EstatePanels' duel). `<html data-motion>` mirrors the answer so `access.css` calms window pops, button pulses, the aura glow, battle entrances, the tower marker, duel pops… when the Master turns it on; the existing `@media (prefers-reduced-motion)` rules still follow the OS. Battles get `.calm` (checked in the browser with the setting on and the OS off).
- **Screen shake** off stops `shake()` and the camera `punch()` in stageFx (the battle juice); **flashes** off hides the skill flare, the wave flash, the summon and ritual whiteouts (CSS under `data-flashes="off"`) and the lobby's lightning.

## 6. Accessibility

- **PixelWindow:** `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, named by its title (`aria-labelledby`), takes focus on open, **Tab / Shift+Tab are trapped** in the topmost window (`focusTrap.ts`), focus returns to the opener on close. Checked with the keyboard in Chromium (40 Tabs never left the Settings window) and in jsdom.
- **DialogBox:** a visually hidden `aria-live="polite"` region speaks each whole line once; the typewriter text is `aria-hidden`; the box is labelled by its speaker.
- `<html lang>` follows the locale. `:focus-visible` gets a gold pixel ring.
- **Locale-aware numbers:** `text.fmtInt` now on the coins in the scene bar and the lobby HUD and the hero chips' CP. French grouping used U+202F, which the pixel fonts lack (it drew "94075"); it is now a plain no-break space ("94 075").
- **Pixel icons** (`src/ui/pixel/icons.ts`, `<PxIcon>`, `<ElementIcon>`, `<ClassIcon>` in `bits.tsx`): the seven elements, the five classes and the untrained, the born trade, gold, gem, menu, sound on/off, settings, keys, save, daily, advice, build, heroes, map, and the English and French flags (no more "GB"/"FR" letters on Windows). Each element and class has its own **silhouette** — `icons.test.ts` requires every pair to differ by ≥ 12 cells, so colour is never the only cue (it caught fire and water at first). Where no canvas draws (tests), the old glyph stands in. Swapped in: ElementBadge, ClassBadge (+ trade chip), the portrait's element pip, the Party Board, the summon lineup, the challenge hero chips, the Codex, the scout's codex note, the Synergy panel, the Game Menu, the scene bar, the lobby HUD, the keyboard help, the backup reminder. `<select>` options lost their emoji (they cannot hold images).
