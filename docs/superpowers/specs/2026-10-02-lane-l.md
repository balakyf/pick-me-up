# Lane L · Living lobby & morale

> **Status:** Done · **Date:** 2026-10-04 · **Base:** 8be101c (wave 5 merged; the worktree started on `main` and was fast-forwarded) · **Worktree branch:** `worktree-agent-a256645ecfe565c01`
> **Plan:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-02-dream-game.md` (pillar 1: visible morale and refusals; pillar 2: grief and drama you can see; pillar 5: morale is a resource, the lobby is where you recover).

Morale is now a resource you can read and act on. Grief plays out over days on the campus. Small incidents happen in the camp, and some of them ask for the Master's word. A Gazette gathers the news beside Isel's letter. Coming home from the tower brings a short camp report with one-click answers. Traits now speak in the lobby.

## What shipped

### 1. Morale: `src/engine/life/morale.ts` (+ `moraleTuning.ts`)

- **Derived, never stored.** `moraleOf(state, heroId)` gives a 0..100 score with five pips and six bands: inspired 5, high 4, steady 3, low 2, shaken 1 and broken 0. Every contribution is a named **factor**, so the UI can say why a hero is low:
  - Sanity: (S − 60) × 0.4.
  - Needs: tired, hungry, lonely and bored are penalties. "Cared for" is +5 when all four are at 60 or more.
  - Floors in a row: the estate's fatigue, from 3 on.
  - Grief: −0.3 per point, capped at −30.
  - Estate states: withdrawn −15, jealous −6.
  - Bonds: friends +3 each and close friends +5 (capped at +10); rivals and grudges weigh against.
  - Recent memories: a clear, a loss, a retreat, a near death, a comrade's fall, guilt, a gift, comfort, a promotion.
  - Camp: a camp incident, a banquet today or yesterday, liking or resenting the job.
  - Trait: Brave/Lionheart +4, Steadfast/Unbreakable +6, the leader family +3.
- **Effect at the ends only.** An inspired hero gets ×1.03 on every magnitude stat. A shaken one gets ×0.96, but not on top of the withdrawn ×0.92. A broken hero **will not deploy**, with the new `DeployReason` `'disheartened'` (counted as a refusal). Steady, high and low change nothing: `applyMorale` returns the same unit, so battles where nobody is at the ends replay bit-identically. The hook is `estate/deploy.moraleAdjust`, so the tower, the raids, the forecast (`prepareFloorBattle`) and true CP all read the same number. The `heroCpFull` memo now keys on the morale multiplier as well.
- **Recovery is something you do.** Rest (needs and fatigue), the Tavern and friends (company eases grief, below), the Infirmary and banquets (Sanity, plus +8/+4 morale), the Memorial (grief), a word or a gift (withdrawal, the gift factor), and answering incidents.
- `campOutlook(state)` gives counts per band, the troubled (shaken or broken, lowest first) and the inspired.

### 2. Grief that plays out (`life.ts`, `react.ts`)

- **A rival's guilt.** When a hero dies, the living with affinity ≤ rival get +20 grief and a `guilt` memory, and the chronicle records it ("can't stop thinking about the last words…").
- **Comfort.** When a grieving hero shares a slot with a friend (affinity ≥ friend), their grief eases by 3 and their Sanity rises by 0.5. The first such moment of a day with heavy grief (≥ 35) is a `consoled` memory and chronicle line.
- **The weekly remembrance.** Every 7 world-days after a death, for up to 8 weeks, each living mourner on the grave gets +15 grief, an `anniversary` memory, and one chronicle line naming everyone who went back to the grave. Mourning visits follow from the grief pull that already exists.
- **Visible.** A tear mark over heroes with grief ≥ 25 on the campus (`ui/world/moodEmote.ts`), mourners walking to the Memorial, the "Grief and comfort" page of the Gazette, and the profile's morale breakdown ("Grieving for Ivo").

### 3. Camp incidents: `src/engine/life/incidents.ts`

There is one roll per life slot, `floatStream(seed, 'incident', slot)`, on the absolute slot clock. Its chance is 0.06, about 2–3 a world-day. It picks among the incidents that are possible right now:

| Kind | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| `brawl` | two awake rivals (≤ −30) in the same place | both −3 Sanity; **waits** (24 slots): *Step in* (+8 affinity, +2 Sanity each) or *Let them settle it* (40 %: +12 affinity, "cleared the air"; else −10, the weaker loses 5 Sanity) |
| `sworn` | close friends (≥ 70) socializing together, once per pair | +10 affinity, a memory for each |
| `nightTraining` | a diligent or Night-Fighter hero, rested, in their own night | **waits** (8): *Send them to bed* (+10 energy) or *Let them train* (15 % of a level of XP, −20 energy) |
| `kitchenFire` | someone in a built Kitchen and a stocked pantry (¼ of the eligible rolls) | half the pantry burns |
| `homesick` | a newcomer (≤ 6 days) with social < 40 | **waits** (36): *Sit with them* (+6 Sanity, +25 social) or *Give them space* (−4 Sanity) |
| `trait` | a hero of the courage, leader, luck, healer, stomach or night family | a rousing word (party +2 Sanity), a drill (yard XP), a found purse, tending the lowest-Sanity hero, a raided pantry, a shared night watch |

- Pending incidents live in an **optional** `LifeState.incidents` (read through `incidentsOf`). The field exists only while something waits, so one long advance equals many short ones (tested). At most 3 wait at once, at most one per hero. An incident whose hero has fallen is dropped.
- **`RESOLVE_INCIDENT { id, choice: 'intervene' | 'let' }`** is a new command (appended). `resolveIncidentWithResult` also returns the outcome. Refusals: `resolveIncident: no such incident`, `…: unknown choice`, `…: a hero involved has fallen`. An unanswered incident settles itself as 'let' when its time is up. A brawl left alone rolls `rngFor(seed, 'incident-let', id)`, so the clock and the Master's "let it be" agree.

### 4. The Gazette: `src/engine/life/gazette.ts`, `src/ui/life/Gazette.tsx`

`gazette(state, since?)` is pure assembly. By default it covers since the last letter or the last 3 world-days, whichever is longer. It returns:
- a **front page** (the highest-weighted item: a death, a masterwork, a sworn bond…);
- sections in order: In memoriam · Grief and comfort · Around the camp · Hearts and bonds · Feuds · Work and craft (12 items each, repeats folded "(×2)");
- the incidents waiting on you, answered in place;
- the **spirits** outlook, with the troubled named and linked to their profiles;
- the **Master XP the heroes' job tier-ups taught** (lane B's seam).

It sits beside Isel's letter rather than replacing it. The letter gains a "Read the Gazette" button, which freezes the Gazette's span before the letter is marked read. There is also a 📰 HUD button with a badge for news since the last letter.

### 5. Back at camp: `src/ui/life/campSummary.ts`, `CampSummary.tsx`

When the lobby mounts after a climb (a module-level "tower mark" of highest/current floor, attempt index and graves), and after the letter if one waits, a short report lists **low spirits, grieving, tired and ready**, each with pips and links to profiles. It offers the commands the game already has, one click each:
- **Rest the tired**: `SET_PARTY` swaps each tired or low party member for the strongest ready hero on the bench, keeping the lines.
- **Hold a banquet**: `BANQUET`.
- **Talk to the withdrawn**: `TALK_TO_HERO`.
- **Visit the Memorial, the Infirmary or the Tavern**: the Master walks there.
- **Read the Gazette.**

### 6. Morale on screen

Pips appear in the tracker rows, the follow bar, the profile and the Party Board (slots and list), and in the tower's party strip, right before deploying. The profile's **morale breakdown** shows the score, the band's effect, the top seven reasons with their points, and up to three "what would help" lines. The Party Board gains a **Disheartened** status. `deployReasonText`/`Fix` cover the new reason, and the war room's warnings pick it up through that seam. On the campus, a storm mark sits over heroes in a waiting incident, a tear over the grieving and a spark over the inspired. Talking to a hero who is caught up in an incident offers "Deal with it". A ⚑ HUD button with a count appears while incidents wait.

### 7. Traits speak, and morale speaks (`src/ui/life/speech.ts`)

`topicsFor` reads `traitOf` (lane J's seam):
- in the party, a Brave/Hot-Blooded/Steadfast/leader hero talks about the next floor;
- awake in their own night, a Night-Fighter says so;
- every family has an any-time line.

Inspired, shaken and broken heroes say so in their own words. The chronicle tells guilt, comfort, the remembrance and every incident outcome. A job tier-up line ends "The Master learned from it (+20 XP)" (lane B's seam). The letter folds several comfort or tier-up lines into one each.

### 8. Ambience (lane H's seam)

The forge rings only while a smith is actually at the anvil (one predicate in `useLobbyAudio.sourcesFor`).

### 9. The bots (`src/sim/sim.ts`)

There is a new profile flag, `camp`: false for casual, true for engaged and whale.
- Attentive bots answer incidents (`RESOLVE_INCIDENT` is a lever). They step into brawls and sit with the homesick, and they let a rested night owl train but send a tired one to bed.
- They feast when two of the top eight are shaken or worse.
- Every bot leaves a disheartened hero off the board in `bestFive`.
- The casual bot lets incidents settle themselves.

## Decisions

- **No schema bump.** Morale is derived. Pending incidents are an optional field read through `incidentsOf`. New memory and chronicle kinds and the deploy reason are additive. The memorial and grief shapes are untouched (lane K reads them).
- **Morale includes Sanity rather than replacing it.** Sanity stays the combat stat it was (the existing ×0.95 / ×0.85 and panic). Morale only adds at the ends, so a frayed hero is not punished twice in the steady band.
- **Romance stays non-romantic** (the lore research: canon has none). The "romance forms" incident is a **sworn bond**: "swore to watch each other's backs on every floor". It reads as devotion, and the Gazette files it under "Hearts and bonds".
- **The camp report shows on every return from a climb**, even when everyone is fine. It is short, and that is when the Master decides who goes next.
- Incidents roll on `floatStream` (the life sim's in-place form of `rngFor`, the same seeding) because they live in the per-slot hot loop. The rare "let it be" draw uses `rngFor`.

## What is left

- **The withdrawn and burnout states** (estate) could fold into morale's language everywhere. The estate's own panels still talk about them separately.
- **More incidents.** Tryout grudges after a duel, a hero refusing a job (the Aaron path), a welcome committee for a newcomer, and incidents tied to weather or the season. The table in `incidents.ts` takes new kinds in one place each.
- **The results screen** (lane K) could show each survivor's morale change and point to the camp report. `moraleOf` and `campOutlook` are ready.
- **The forecast's alternatives** (lane C) could offer "rest the tired", using `restSwap` from `ui/life/campSummary.ts`, as an odds-changer. A port to the engine would be small.
- **Tavern music** (lane H): the ambience could add a music-corner source when heroes play music at the Tavern. That needs a new ambience voice, so I left it.
- **Balance** (lane R): inspired is reachable for any well-kept hero with friends at full Sanity, about a third of a healthy engaged roster in the browser save. If lane R wants it rarer, raise `MORALE.bands.inspired` or trim `cared` and `friendsCap`.
- **The backup reminder banner** (App) sits on top of the lobby's follow bar at the same `top: 70px`. This is pre-existing and was seen during the check.
