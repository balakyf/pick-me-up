## What shipped, brief item by item

1. **Effectiveness, full pack.** `popupStyle.ts` (pure, tested) plans every popup:
   - colour = the blow's element (`blowElement`: the skill's element, else the striker's; plain physical reads warm white);
   - size = share of the target's max HP: 9 stage px for a scratch, 12 at 10 %, 21 from 40 % up; a crit is 25 % bigger (14–24) and keeps its hit-stop (the number lands after the freeze); a crowded sweep shrinks (8 numbers read at about half size);
   - a phone never reads a number below 12 CSS px or a tag below 9 (the minimum is divided by the stage zoom);
   - CRITICAL! / WEAK! / RESIST are separate badges (WEAK! is a warm orange plate, RESIST a slate one); IMMUNE is a plated word.
   - **The elements hint** (`ElementsHint.tsx`, `elementsHint.ts`): the first time a WEAK hit lands, a card explains the wheel, built from the engine's `ELEMENT_ADVANTAGE` and multipliers so it can never drift ("Fire › Wind › Earth › Water › Fire · Light ⇄ Dark", ×1.5 / ×0.75). "Got it" sets `localStorage['pmu.hint.elements']` (every access wrapped). It sits in the sky under the caption on desktop, over the windows on a phone (the stage keeps its size), and steps aside during a hero's death moment.
2. **AoE beats.** `battleFrames.beatRanges`: a run of consecutive blows/misses/guards/heals on one tick carrying two or more blows plays as ONE beat (with the foes it fells). A sweep holds `560 + 60·(n−1)` ms (stagger capped at 480) `+ 260` if something fell, instead of 460 ms per target. Hero and escort deaths split the run and still hold the scene (2.6 s, never rushed); in a trial a hero who drops is only out, so it folds. Frames now carry `from/to` (their event range), `targets` and `sp`; `frameAtEvents`/`eventsThrough` map frames ↔ events so a mid-battle order still resumes from the frame on screen (`orderResume` unchanged). Each target flinches and its sparks fly in step with its number (`useImpactJuice.ts`). The old BattleScene/battleFrames tests still pass; new ones pin the grouping deliberately (and check on real logs that every event plays exactly once and no sweep spans two acts).
3. **Popup lanes.** Every kind goes through one plan: hits, heals, misses, guards, IMMUNE and the new HP-cost popup ("−70 HP" over the caster). `choreo.popupPlaces` lifts a box the least it can and, on the way, tries thirds of its width sideways, so a sweep's column fans out instead of towering; numbers also keep clear of the skill banner over its caster and stay on the visible stretch of a cropped stage. A test plans every kind at three zooms and finds no overlap.
4. **Skill banner + cut-in.** The skill's name stays over its caster through every blow of its act (and hangs inward at the stage's edge). `SkillCutIn`: an element-coloured band sweeps across the stage with the hero's bust and the skill name; it lengthens the cast by 520 ms. **Decision:** a grade B skill is cut in the first time each hero casts it in a fight, an A-or-better skill every time (`cutInActs`) — otherwise a late fight was a string of cut-ins (+8 s median). Skipped at 4× and under reduced motion. Sizes use container units (`cqh`/`cqw`) so they scale with the stage, not the window.
5. **Ghost HP.** `HpBar.tsx`: the bar drops at once, a pale ghost lingers and drains after ~0.45 s (speed-scaled), on the stage, in the party rows, the foe window and the escort pane.
6. **SP + turn order.** Party rows have an SP bar (maxSP less each cast's `spCost`). `turnOrder.ts` replays the ATB gauges from `CombatUnitInit.spd` along the log (Gale speed from the `floor-mods` event; turns the log doesn't show — escorts, sleeping giants, fizzles — inferred as silent turns; a looming foe is dormant until its `wakes` beat) and runs them forward. `TurnStrip` shows the current actor and the next 6 (5 on a phone): hero busts, foe sprites cropped to head and shoulders. **Test on the bots' real logs (101 battles, F1–F68):** the next actor matches every logged turn, the whole strip matches until someone falls or a wave arrives, zero divergences.
7. **Waves.** 'WAVE 2/3' slams in on wave-spawn (and 'WAVE 1/3' at the start of a multi-wave fight); 'WAVE 1/3 CLEARED' with a flash on the `wave-cleared` beat (no flash under reduced motion).
8. **Objective HUD.** `objectives.ts` (pure) + `ObjectiveHud.tsx`: the canon label (Escort, Survival…) and plain lines — Defeat every foe (— N waves) / Survive until the bell / Hold off N waves / Escape — reach the exit / Keep X alive / Defeat X / Take the prize from X — each ticking ✔ or ✖; a survival clock, the road to the exit (hero turns, corrected by the engine's quarter beats), the escort's HP pane, the wave count, and "The horde is spent" on that beat. Objective units wear a crown (targets) or a shield (escorts) on the stage and in the foe window. On desktop it is the left of a top bar `mission · caption · turn order` (a grid, so it never covers the caption); on a phone upright it docks above the stage; on its side, in the side column. A test checks every won real battle ends with all its lines met.
9. **Hit-stop.** The first crit of a beat freezes the FX and adds `.hitstop` to the stage, which pauses sprite idles, backdrop drift, turn marks and crowns for `HITSTOP_MS`.
10. **Phone battle.** `battleFx.battleLayout`: `wide` / `narrow` (upright phone) / `beside` (phone on its side). `useStageFit.ts`: on a phone the stage crops to the stretch the units stand on (`unitSpan`), upright it also draws the two sides in (`NARROW_SQUEEZE` 0.82), and `fitStage(…, minW)` zooms in with eighth steps under 2×. 390×844: units ~33×44 CSS px (were 24×32), controls in one thumb row (44 px), foes in two columns, mission above and turn order below the stage. 844×390: the stage fills the height; the controls lead the side column so Pause, speeds, Skip and orders never need a scroll. No horizontal scroll at any size checked.

BattleScene stays the orchestrator: the layout went to `useStageFit.ts`, the juice to `useImpactJuice.ts`, popup inputs to `popupStyle.beatPopups/flinchDelays`. New CSS is in `battleRead.css` (nothing in `ui.css`); `battle.css` only had its caption selectors moved into the top bar.

## For the merge with lane F (and later lanes)

- **`src/sim/fightStats.replayMs`** still times one event per frame. It should call `battleFrames.replayLength(log)` (exported, same units) so the combat-feel gate measures what the scene plays. Lane F owns `src/sim`, so I left it.
- **SP regen:** the SP bar is maxSP − Σ`spCost` of the unit's acts. If lane F regenerates SP or adds SP events, `battleFrames.step` should read them (one case in the switch).
- **Stuns / haste:** `turnOrder.ts` knows Gale. A stun that costs a turn will show as a silent turn and resynchronise (counted in `divergences`); a speed buff will make the forecast drift until it is read from its event. Both are one case in `replayGauges` once the events exist. Multi-hit skills already fold into one beat with numbers stacked in lanes.
- **Hooks:** lane F's StatusIcons/statusCaptions mount points in `UnitSprite.tsx`, `PartyRows.tsx` and `battleFrames.ts` (the `default:` branch of `step`) are where they were; `PartyRows` gained a `party-bars` wrapper around the HP bar.
- `DURATION`, `eventDuration`, `HERO_DEATH_MS`, `HITSTOP_MS` keep their values and exports.
