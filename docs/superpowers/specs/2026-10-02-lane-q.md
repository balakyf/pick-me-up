## What shipped

### 1 · PvP on stage (`engine/pvp/*`, `ui/pvp/*`, `ui/pvpPanels.tsx`)

**Engine (deterministic, through `reduce`):**

- **An invasion keeps its battle.** `InvasionRecord` gains optional `rivalId`, `guildId` and `replay: PvpReplay` (seed, floor, the defenders as they stood with the Tactical Center's bonus, the raiders). `pvpReplayLog(replay)` fights it again, identically. Only the newest `PVP_STAGE.replaysKept` (3) log lines keep a replay (about 29 KB each on a whale save). No schema bump: the fields are optional and old records simply have no replay.
- **A hand-picked team.** `RAID_RIVAL` and `COUNTER_RAID` take optional `heroIds` (1–5). `teamSlots` puts each picked hero on their party line, or their class's line when they are off the board. Refusals: `teamRefusal` (empty, more than five, a repeat, someone unfit) and `counterRaidRefusal` (its old messages kept). `counterRaid` now also returns its battle and the captor (`captorOf`). `counterRaidWithResult` is new, and `raidWithResult` takes the team.
- **The guild raid names its guildmates.** `guildmates(state)` lists the simulated rivals of your sector whose guild is yours, borrowing from lower sectors when there are too few. `mateShares` splits the mates' damage between them on a stream of its own (`guildmateShares`), as integers that sum exactly to `mates`. The outcome carries `log` and `roster`.
- **The server war returns its battles:** `battles: { log, won, ratio }[]` and `enemyGuildId`.

No draw that existed moved: the sim is identical (below).

**UI:**

- **`PvpStageHost` + `stageBus`** (mounted in `App`) play a fight from anywhere. The rival's title card (`RivalCardView`) shows a swinging guild banner, the handle slamming down, the guild, floor, rating and a WHALE tag. It leaves after 2.6 s (1.3 s at 2×/4×), or on a click, Esc, Space or Enter. Then each battle plays in the shared `BattleScene`, non-lethal, with its own closing words: THE DEFENSE HELD / FELL, RAID WON / DRIVEN BACK, FREED, THE COLOSSUS FALLS / TIME!, BATTLE WON / LOST. BattleScene gained an optional `banner` prop, and `battleFrames` a `fight` option ("{name} is out of the fight.", "The fight is over. Nobody dies here.").
- **Invasions:**
  - `InvasionAlarm` appears in the lobby for an invasion you have not watched: who came, their guild, how it went, the gold lost, with **Watch** and **Later**. It waits while any window is open. Which invasions were seen is stored per account in localStorage.
  - Every log row with a replay has **Watch ▸**, and log days are now the account's days.
- **The raid sheet (`RaidSheet`):**
  - the rival's crest, guild and blurb;
  - their defense against your team ("about ×1.13 your team's strength · an even fight", with both CPs);
  - the stakes: loot, the kidnap chance, rating, Sanity, "nobody dies";
  - the team as chips, picked in lane N's `HeroPicker`, whose refusals come from the deploy rails.
  The same sheet is the **counter-raid** for a held hero.
- **The captive board (`CaptiveBoard`):**
  - The chain in five steps.
  - Each held hero: their bust, "held by {master}" with the captor's crest and guild, a bar of the hold running out ("synthesized in 10 world-h", red under a day), the ransom with what you can't yet pay in red, **Pay the ransom**, and **Counter-raid…**.
  - Each hero you took: grades, ransom back, and the dark path (synthesize into a hero chosen in the shared picker, with the favor cost spelled out).
- **The Defense tab** uses the shared picker too.

## Decisions

- **The invasion's battle is stored, not reconstructed.** The heroes change after the night they defended, so only a snapshot replays honestly. Three replays bound the save.
- **The stage is a host above everything** (like lane N's sheet host), so a fight can start from a window, the lobby or the guild hall. Battle and card listen first (capture), so the window underneath never closes on Esc.
- **PvP plays non-lethal.** The fallen kneel like a trial's, but the words are the fight's own. "Nobody dies in PvP" is the canon rule (Layer 4).
- **Upgrades are shown by marks, not by redrawing the building per level.** This is consistent across buildings, and readable at the lobby's zoom.
- **Seen invasions are per browser** (a convenience, in localStorage), so the save needs no new field for them.

## What is left

- **Facades:** the tile renderer still draws every front wall alike. Per-building facades (timber, stone, columns) and doors would complete the architecture.
- **The guild raid sheet takes the party as it stands.** A team picker (like the raid sheet's) would need `GUILD_RAID { heroIds }`. The same goes for the server war.
- **Bots never raid rivals or watch fights.** A PvP lever for lane R would be `RAID_RIVAL` with the strongest five (`teamSlots`) once a week.
- **The rival's title card is generic for every rival.** A bust of the rival Master (the master avatar generator seeded by `rival.id`) would make them people.
- **Old saves.** Invasions resolved before this lane have no replay (no Watch button). Counter-raid records from before carry no guild.
- **The lobby HUD at ≥1600** uses CSS `zoom` (supported by Chromium and Firefox ≥126; Safari scales it too). A layout pass of the corner cards themselves would be finer.
- **Two-column floor list at ≥1600.** Not done: the list is a column-reverse flex, and a grid would invert its order.
