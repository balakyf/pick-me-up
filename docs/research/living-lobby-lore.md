# Living Lobby Lore: Heroes and the Waiting Room (대기실)

Companion to `PICK-ME-UP-GAME-BIBLE.md` and `pick-me-up-raw-facts.json`. This file
records only what is **new** relative to those two files, or it restates a known fact
only when a new detail hangs off it. The subject is what makes the heroes and the
waiting room feel alive: facilities, autonomy, personalities, grief, morale and work
assignment.

## How this was researched, and how far to trust it

- **Sources were reached indirectly.** The egress proxy blocked every direct fetch of
  fandom (pick-me-up / infinite-gacha), namu.wiki (ko and en), novelupdates, webnovel,
  reddit, novelhall and novelfire. All findings come from **search-engine result
  summaries** of those pages, so each is a paraphrase of a wiki line or a chapter
  excerpt, not a first-hand read. Cited URLs are the pages the summaries were drawn
  from.
- **Confidence tags:**
  - `[high]`: several independent pages agree, or a wiki states it outright.
  - `[medium]`: one wiki or chapter snippet says it.
  - `[low]`: an inference, or a snippet that is garbled or contradicts another.
- **Name reconciliation.** One character has many romanizations:
  - **Belkist = Velkist = Belquist = 벨키스트.** "Bell" is his nickname.
  - **Iolka = Eolka.**
  - **Edith = Edis.** Her surname appears as Karlen or Callen.
  - **Nerissa = Nerisa.** Her surname appears as Iyor, Eor or Eeyore.
  - **Jenna = Zena = Xena = Jena.** Her surname appears as Cirai or Shirai.
  - **Aaron's** surname appears as Delcut or Delkirt.
  - **Dika = Deeka.**
  - **Catio = Katio.**
  - **Sirris = Sirres.** Her surname appears as Adenthem or Azenthem.
  - **Yurneth = Yulnet.** Her surname appears as Syd or Sid.
  - **Lidigyon = Ridigeon.**
  - **Iselle = Isel = Eselle.**
  - **Amkena = Anytng.**
  - **Taonier = Townia = Taonere.**
- **Names from the brief that were not found.** Searches in English and Korean
  returned **no trace** of *Aileen, Karin, Rinne, Charles, Illuminia, Faylan, Brigid*
  or *Rakan* as characters in this work. Treat them as non-canon. If the game needs
  them, present them as original characters. "Rakan" may be a garbled form of
  **Rakari**, a beastman in Party 3 whose name appears only in the raw facts.
  `[medium]`
- **No romance in the novel.** Fandom summaries say plainly that **the novel has
  zero romance**. There is teasing and banter, and some scenes read like a harem
  setup, but no romantic plot follows. Build relationship systems around
  **comradeship, mentorship, rivalry and grief**, not courtship. `[medium]`
  - Source: https://infinite-gacha.fandom.com/wiki/Islat_Han/Relationships

## 1. The Waiting Room: facilities, upgrades, staffing and growth

### 1.1 New facility facts (not in the bible)

| Facility | New detail | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| **Restaurant / Kitchen** | Early Townia has only **baked potatoes**. A story beat marks the restaurant upgrade: **bread replaces baked potatoes on the table**. A wiki summary says **a larger variety of food lowers the waiting room's stress level**. | medium |
| **Smithy (대장간)** | A small brazier with a blazing fire, an anvil and hand tools. Three crafting modes: **Enhancement** (reinforce an existing weapon), **Casting** (make a new weapon from materials) and **Commission** (hand the whole job to an artisan). Output is limited by a **"triple penalty"**: low facility level, **no assistant artisans** and **no blueprint**. The fairy mentions "ultra-special weapons" that need all three conditions met. | high |
| **Training Center** | The core facility. Training **does not raise stats or level**. It refines skills and skill learning. It later gains **three annexes: Magic Lab, Alchemy and Library**. Heroes "never neglect training" and stay in the camp all day. | high |
| **Magic Hall (마법관)** | Built by **merging the three Training Center annexes** (Magic Lab, Alchemy, Library). Inside: dim lanterns, incense and the smell of old books. It opens a **research tech tree**. **Research points** come from **placing personnel in the laboratory**, and they are spent to **upgrade the waiting room**. | high |
| **Research tech tree** (three branches) | (1) **Hero Responsiveness Study** improves how heroes respond to missions and commands. (2) **Facility Scalability Study** opens each facility's detail tab and allows more direct operation. (3) **Intensive Dungeon Research** adds special-purpose dungeon types and steadies the material supply. The branches map to **hero / facility / dungeon**. | medium |
| **Plaza (1st-floor square)** | Heroes wait on benches here while the Master is connected. It also hosts affiliation changes, such as heroes being called up for new parties. | medium |
| **Sparring arena** | Sits in or near the plaza. Duels use **real weapons** and draw blood, for example in the Edith vs Usher tryout. | medium |
| **Vault, upper floor (memorial)** | Holds **memorial tablets** for the dead. Edith's belongings were set **beside Iolka's memorial tablet**. This is the in-story "relic" room. | high |
| **Promotion window** | Promotion is shown as a public event. The "Reporting Ceremony" chapter has **three coloured attribute stones spinning** in the synthesis window, then white light and a **fireworks sound** when the Master taps *Yes*. Heroes watch from the plaza. | medium |
| **Dormitory** | A search summary mentions **heroes voicing dissatisfaction with the dormitory**, plus fairy tips on improving facilities. No floor plan was found. | low |
| **Bath / infirmary / hospital** | **Not found** in any source. Any version the game uses is an original design. | n/a |
| **Airship dock and shipyard** (Taonier, late game) | The airship **"Capitalism"** docks here. A **second airship is being built after it, but at least five are needed**. After the story ends, Catio keeps studying airships. | medium |

### 1.2 How the space grows (story timeline)

1. **Tutorial and early Townia.** About 11 first residents. There are baked potatoes,
   a low-level smithy with no artisans, and a training ground with a running track.
   Arc titles "Laying the Groundwork" and "Building internal stability" cover
   ch. 12–16. `[high]`
2. **Food and training upgrades.** The restaurant starts serving bread, and the
   Training Center gains annexes. `[medium]`
3. **Magic Hall merge.** The three annexes fuse into the Magic Hall, and the research
   tree opens (around ch. 50–53). From here, staffing the lab becomes a way to
   upgrade the base. `[high]`
4. **Party tryouts** (ch. 70–71, "Tryouts"). Real-weapon sparring on the plaza
   decides party slots, and the Master reassigns affiliations that evening. `[high]`
5. **Stratified floors.** Taonier grows to 4 residential floors: trainees, then
   support staff and instructors, then the raid team, then Parties 1–2 (the layout is
   already in the bible). New insight: **floor placement signals status**. The
   guaranteed 5★ recruit **Wiryung** was placed straight onto the **4th floor**
   because "it wouldn't make sense to treat her the same as other new recruits".
   `[medium]`
6. **Airship era.** The dock, the airship *Capitalism* and a second hull under
   construction. `[medium]`
7. **Webtoon eps. 148–164** show a **reconstruction of the waiting room** and **a
   sector-exploration team recruiting heroes from other waiting rooms**, told partly
   from Edith's and Nerissa's points of view. `[medium]`
   - Source: https://namu.wiki/w/%ED%94%BD%20%EB%AF%B8%20%EC%97%85!(%EC%9B%B9%ED%88%B0) (via search summary)

### 1.3 Who staffs the base

- **Fairy administrator Iselle** relays between heroes and the Master, posts
  **tips**, and runs the tutorial. New details:
  - She is **Moebius's 100,000,479th copy**.
  - She **adores Loki** and posts tips built on Han's advice "because she doesn't
    want to get on his wrong side".
  - She **can kill heroes who defy her**: she killed **Carl Molmont**, the first 2★
    shown, "in self defense" when he went against her command, and she wiped out the
    **Pulverizing Wolves** mercenary party.
  - She helps Han **forge weapons and potions through the system** and **deletes the
    logs of his personal outings**, so the Master never sees them.
  - Confidence: `[high]`
  - Sources:
    - https://infinite-gacha.fandom.com/wiki/Iselle
    - https://infinite-gacha.fandom.com/wiki/Carl_Molmont
    - https://infinite-gacha.fandom.com/wiki/Anytng
- **Sub-master and proxy chain in Taonier.** Amkena makes **Han** her sub-master
  out of favoritism. Later, **Nerissa** leaves Party 1 of her own accord to become
  **Vice-Master's Proxy** and run Taonier's administration, "allowing Han to focus
  entirely on the Tower". `[high]`
  - https://pick-me-up.fandom.com/wiki/Nerissa_Iyor
  - https://infinite-gacha.fandom.com/wiki/Nerissa_Eor
- **Training Center head: Roderick.** He is a burned-out veteran. See §3. `[high]`
- **Training instructor: Dika/Deeka.** A 1★ original who became a Taonier training
  instructor. `[medium]`
  - https://pick-me-up.fandom.com/wiki/Deeka
- **Trainer of the other groups: Edith** (Party 2 leader). `[high]`
- **Researcher: Iolka.** She is the only mage early on (mages come only from
  gacha), so she naturally staffs the Magic Hall. `[medium]`
  - https://infinite-gacha.fandom.com/wiki/Eolka_Rivel_Strashur
- **Support heroes (1★ non-combatants).** Wiki: *"responsible for various tasks
  around Townia, such as cooking, carpentering or forging... filler characters...
  storylines rarely developed."* Also: *"Most 1-star heroes are far from fighting
  because they were summoned while going about their daily lives."* So cooks and
  smiths are ordinary people pulled in mid-life, not warriors. **No named cook or
  blacksmith turned up in any source.** `[high]`
  - https://infinite-gacha.fandom.com/wiki/Pick_Me_Up!/Heroes
- **Niflheim staff** (Loki's original base):
  - **Ecle/Eclat Pardisathe** (6★) is **Training Director for all education**. She
    was a captured enemy hero. Sirris proposed executing her, but Loki absorbed her
    instead, and she rose through the ranks.
  - **Elderkin Bragh** is **Advanced Instructor** (rank 114).
  - **Nithledd/Nisled** is Yurneth's **secretary**, living on floor 13.
  - Niflheim runs an **education service with job-change processes**. Of every
    **100 trainees, the top 3 transfer into Niflheim proper**.
  - Confidence: `[medium]`
  - Sources:
    - https://pick-me-up.fandom.com/wiki/Ecle_Pardisathe
    - https://infinite-gacha.fandom.com/wiki/Elderkin_Bragh
    - https://infinite-gacha.fandom.com/wiki/Niflheimr
- A search summary refers to **"a waiting room where seniors train heroes
  personally"**. In other words, senior heroes mentor juniors directly. `[low]`

## 3. Named heroes: personalities, quirks, relationships and base jobs

### 3.1 Taonier / Townia (Master: Amkena/Anytng; de facto leader: Han)

**Han Islat (Loki, Han Seojin)**
- Personality: calm, rational and pragmatic. **Cold to strangers and to people he
  thinks are useless. Kind to comrades, but never says so.** Rewards merit and
  punishes uselessness.
- Quirk: he received a **wooden warhorse statue** from Amkena when he wanted food,
  and was angry about it. She kept sending them, he came to accept them, and he now
  **wants to collect 1,000**. This makes a lovely running gag.
- Base jobs: sub-master (from Amkena's favoritism), and forger in the smithy despite
  the triple penalty.
- Confidence: `[high]`
- Sources:
  - https://infinite-gacha.fandom.com/wiki/Islat_Han
  - https://infinite-gacha.fandom.com/wiki/Anytng

**Jenna (Zena) Cirai**
- Background: a 1★ **genius** who was rated only 1★ because she grew up in a forest
  with her father. Class: Hunter / Archer / Thief.
- Personality: **kind and easygoing**. She makes sure every newcomer understands
  their situation, which is the "welcome committee" role. She **gets over deaths
  quickly for her own survival**.
- Relationships: grew nearly as fast as Han, and is **one of the few people Han
  trusts**. Their bond is built on teasing.
- Personality-database type: ISFP.
- Arc: with Han from floor 1 to the end. Nihaku was her teacher (already in the
  bible).
- Confidence: `[high]`
- Source: https://infinite-gacha.fandom.com/wiki/Jenna_Cirai

**Aaron Delcut** (spearman, 1★)
- Personality: **timid, holds back, very hardworking, low self-esteem**. He falls
  behind Han and Jenna despite training.
- Relationships: loses his Party 1 seat to Velkist in a duel.
- Arc:
  1. Han suggests he become an instructor. Aaron refuses.
  2. Han sends him to **Muden**, who scolds him.
  3. He is moved to **Niflheim's Training Center**, where he learns intermediate
     spearmanship.
  4. He enters the **Raid Festa** in Niflheim uniform.
  5. He becomes **"the only case where a 1-star became stronger beyond the limits
     of his talent."**
- Personality-database type: ESFP.
- Confidence: `[high]`
- Sources:
  - https://pick-me-up.fandom.com/wiki/Aaron_Delkirt
  - https://www.webnovel.com/book/pick-me-up-infinite-gacha_32589943708204605/179.-just-for-once-(3)_94671205260385378

**Velkist / Belkist / Belquist** (native 2★ swordsman / berserker)
- Appearance: light purple hair, red eyes, tall, and a **scar from the eye down the
  face**.
- Personality: **barbaric, fearless, aggressive, determined, patient, a "fighting
  fanatic"**. He trained from the moment he arrived. His first line to the resentful
  party: *"Why the glares? Looks like you want to eat us alive. Just came to say
  hello."*
- Relationships: **won Aaron's Party 1 seat by duel**. Late in the story he helps
  Jenna save Han. After the reset he **follows Jenna to Valhalla "because he was
  bored"**.
- Base role: sparring partner and rival. No crafting role was found.
- Confidence: `[high]`
- Sources:
  - https://infinite-gacha.fandom.com/wiki/Velkist
  - https://pick-me-up.fandom.com/wiki/Belkist

**Nerissa Iyor** (native 2★, later 3★ rogue; rapier and poisoned hidden blades)
- Background: fallen nobility, sold into slavery, and branded by the Halgion family
  with the **Twilight Seal**. It is a rose mark on her waist that **enforces
  absolute obedience and boosts her abilities, at the cost of lifespan**.
- Personality: **cold and analytical**.
- Crafting: she **brews her own paralysis poison**, a natural fit for an alchemy or
  crafting job.
- Base roles: from floor 16 she scouts by stealth. Later she is **Vice-Master's
  Proxy, running Taonier's administration**.
- Confidence: `[high]`
- Source: https://pick-me-up.fandom.com/wiki/Nerissa_Iyor

**Iolka (Eolka) Rivel Strashur** (3★ elemental mage)
- Appearance: ginger hair tied back, red robe.
- Base role: **Taonier's researcher** and the main attacker of Party 1.
- Death: she **dies of mana overload on floor 35 (ch. 142), saving Party 1**.
- Aftermath: her **memorial tablet sits in the vault**. After the ending reset she
  is **revived without her memories**. Han apologizes: *"You died back there because
  of my mistake... I wanted to apologize if we ever met again."* She joins Valhalla.
- Fans joke that Catio (4★) replaced her at level 31 even though she had been there
  since level 1. That is a displacement sting.
- Personality-database type: INFJ.
- Confidence: `[high]`
- Sources:
  - https://pick-me-up.fandom.com/wiki/Iolka_Rivel_Strachur
  - https://x.com/0xEniola/status/1878230999009509861

**Edith (Edis) Karlen/Callen** (3★ rogue, dual knives)
- Background: ex-**Pulverizing Wolves Mercenaries**.
- Personality: **seems cold and aloof but is surprisingly affectionate**. A practical

- Base roles: **Party 2 leader and trainer of the other groups**.

### 3.2 Niflheim (Loki's original account; Vice-Master Sirris)

- **Sirris Adenthem.** Rank 1, **Vice-Master and sub-master**, leader of the main
  attack squad. Mature, blonde, green-eyed. She **ran Niflheim in Loki's absence**
  and **proposed executing the captured Eclat**. Protective hard-liner. `[high]`
  - https://pick-me-up.fandom.com/wiki/Sirris_Azenthem
- **Yurneth Syd.** Rank 2, "The King's Eyes", a space and magic scholar. **Calm,
  diligent, gentle-faced, but an "ominous smile crosses her face whenever she feels
  someone has wronged Loki."** Lives on floor 13 with her secretary Nithledd.
  `[high]`
  - https://pick-me-up.fandom.com/wiki/Yulnet_Sid
- **Lidigyon / Ridigeon.** Rank 3, a pure swordsman. Silver hair and scars. **Calm

## 4. Reactions to death, favoritism, synthesis and low morale

### 4.1 Death of comrades

- **Floor 35 is the canonical mass-casualty event.** Iolka dies of mana overload,
  and all of Kishasha's beastmen die. Three different trauma responses follow:
  - **Kishasha withdraws completely** ("shut himself up like a ruined person"). He
    is recovered by the leader's persuasion. `[high]`
  - **Roderick burns out and tries to retire.** A peer's all-night talk turns him
    into an instructor, a non-combat job. `[high]`
  - **Han carries guilt** and apologizes at a group drink with the original four,
    then again years later to the revived, memory-less Iolka. `[high]`
- **Jenna copes by moving on quickly.** This survival trait is written as part of
  her character. `[high]`
- **Memorialization.** Tablets and belongings go to the **vault's upper floor**.
  Edith's things sit beside Iolka's tablet. `[high]`
- **Heroic sacrifice buffs the living.** Edith's rousing speech on floor 50 raised
  every member's stats, and her self-destruct saved the evacuees. `[high]`
- **Death as a tryout outcome.** Usher, who lost the tryout duel, dies on floor 20.
  Weaker heroes pushed into combat die early. `[medium]`

### 4.2 The Master's favoritism

- **Amkena overfocuses on Han** and neglects the other party members during
  missions. Sources say the favoritism "appears to create tension with other
  heroes", but no specific jealous scene surfaced. `[medium]`
- **Status by floor.** Heroes live on floors by rank, and a new 5★ skipping straight
  to floor 4 is framed as obviously deserved, not resented. `[medium]`
- **Gifts can backfire.** Repetitive or poor gifts lower favorability (already in
  the bible). Han's shift from anger at the warhorse statue to fondness shows that
  **the same gift can change meaning over time**. `[high]`

### 4.3 Synthesis

- **Shay** was fused into Han by accident, and she **accepted it calmly and
  encouraged him**. This is the dignified-sacrifice template. `[high]`
- Synthesis "is effective in controlling heroes but is bad for morale." Masters use
  it **as punishment for refusal or uselessness** (폐기, disposal). `[high]`
- Loki's refusal to synthesize is a defining trait, and it buys loyalty strong
  enough that his heroes **searched for him for months** after he vanished. `[high]`

### 4.4 Low morale and stress

- **Food variety lowers stress.** The base has a **stress level** that more varied
  food reduces. `[medium]`
- The waiting room "has facilities for reducing stress and entertainment beyond
  combat". `[medium]`
  - Source: search summary of namu.wiki 설정
- **The truth crushes morale.** If heroes learn the goddess treats Taonians as toys,
  morale drops sharply, so Han **hides it**. `[high]`
- **Fear-based rule is fragile.** "A lobby controlled with fear is bound to fall."
  `[high]`
- **Morale tools a Master has:** treats, banquets, glow sticks, gifts,
  screenshots, sub-master appointments and warhorse statues. `[high]`

## Design hooks

- **Stress meter per floor or dorm.** Food variety, facility level and recent deaths
  feed it. Upgrading the kitchen from potatoes to bread to banquets is the first
  visible "the base is alive" milestone.
- **Magic Hall research tree with three branches:**
  - Responsiveness: fewer ignored orders and faster command response.
  - Facility Scalability: unlocks each building's detail tab and manual control.
  - Dungeon Research: new daily-dungeon types and steadier materials.
  - Research points scale with the number and quality of staff in the lab.
- **The smithy's triple penalty as the tutorial for staffing.** Show three greyed
  requirement icons (facility level, assistant artisan, blueprint). Offer three
  modes: Enhancement, Casting and Commission. Commission gives an assigned hero's
  output, while hands-on modes are minigames whose skill improves with practice.
- **Job tags drawn from canon archetypes:** Instructor, Head of Training, Proxy
  Administrator, Researcher, Alchemist, Engineer, Smith, Cook, Carpenter,
  Secretary, Welcomer. Retired or traumatized fighters convert to Instructor (the
  Roderick path) instead of being discarded.
- **Career-choice events.** A struggling hero may **refuse** a suggested non-combat
  job (the Aaron path). The player can then send them to train under a mentor for N
  days. On return they gain a "beyond talent" growth bonus and a loyalty spike.
- **Tryout duels.** A plaza sparring arena with real weapons decides party slots.
  The loser takes an injury or morale hit and may harbour rivalry (Velkist vs
  Aaron).
- **Floor-35-style trauma states:**
  - *Withdrawn*: refuses missions until a leader persuades them. Needs a high-affinity
    hero or the player.
  - *Burnout*: requests retirement. A peer can argue them into a staff role.
  - *Survivor's guilt*: triggers a "drink at the bar" scene with the surviving
    original members.
- **Memorial vault.** Dead heroes' tablets and belongings go on display. Visiting
  gives a small morale recovery, and a memorial wall that fills up is powerful
  feedback.
- **Last-stand speech.** A leader-type hero near death can deliver a party-wide stat
  buff (Edith on floor 50). Optionally, a self-sacrifice that saves everyone else.
- **Favoritism tracker.** When the player repeatedly picks, gifts or focuses one
  hero, others' morale drifts down and a "the Master only looks at him" line
  appears. Loki's creed ("a lobby ruled by fear will fall") is the counterweight: no
  synthesis gives a loyalty bonus.
- **Gift meaning drift.** Repeating a gift first annoys a hero, then after a
  threshold becomes their beloved collection (the warhorse statue, with a goal of
  1,000). Show it on a shelf in their room.
- **Welcome committee.** Newly summoned heroes gain a faster "acclimation" if a
  Welcomer-trait hero (Jenna) is present. Otherwise they may arrive despondent (the
  Wiryung path), with a temporary performance penalty.
- **Floor-based housing hierarchy.** Residence floor follows rank and party. A
  high-star newcomer can skip floors, which risks resentment only if the player
  over-favours them.
- **The fairy as an enforcer NPC.** Iselle relays hero requests and posts tips, but
  a hero pushed to open defiance of the fairy can be killed by her (the Molmont
  path). Use this as a hard-fail warning for the player's mismanagement.
- **Offline report on login.** A digest in "report format" of what heroes did while
  the player was away: training reps, duels, drinks, outings, complaints about the
  dorm, and a newly crafted item.
- **Mentor bonds across bases.** Late game, one hero can be seconded to an allied
  base's training center (Aaron to Niflheim), returning later with new skills.
- **Secret truth meter.** Some lore reveals lower the whole base's morale if heroes
  learn them. The player can choose to hide or reveal them, which raises trust
  (Nerissa) or morale (the Taonians).
- **Ex-enemy recruitment.** Captured heroes from PvP (Eclat, the rank-9 hero) can be
  executed (a Sirris-style hard-liner proposes it) or converted. Converted heroes
  climb to staff roles over time.
- **Keep it non-romantic.** Canon has zero romance. Relationship tiers should read
  as trust, rivalry and mentorship, which fits the source and avoids tonal mismatch.
