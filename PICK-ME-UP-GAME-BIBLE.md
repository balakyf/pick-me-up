## Executive Summary
"Pick Me Up, Infinite Gacha!" is an in-story mobile gacha tower-climber where the player ("Master") summons Heroes via an "infinite gacha" (no duplicates), fortifies a personal base/lobby ("waiting room"), and dispatches parties of five to clear a roguelike 100-floor Tower stage-by-stage. Mechanically it rests on three pillars: Mobius Summon (every pull is a unique hero who "actually exists"), Quanton/Quantum AI (heroes behave with human-like autonomy and free will when the Master is offline), and a permadeath roguelike Tower whose difficulty spikes every 5 floors and explodes at floor 80. Heroes occupy a 1-7 star rarity ladder with hard per-star level caps (1★=Lv10 up to 6★=Lv99, 7★=100+); gacha tops out at 5★, 6★ requires promotion, and 7★ requires the rare "Book of Reverse Heaven." Two systems grow heroes — Promotion (rank-up via elemental/upgrade stones, granting new skills) and Synthesis (sacrificing one hero into another, lossy and morale-damaging) — and a hero, once dead in battle or consumed by synthesis, is gone forever. Per-account randomization (different enemies, maps, NPCs, summon odds, and missions per Master) plus a base economy of Gold (soft) and Gems (premium), daily dungeons for materials, and PvP "dimensional rift" invasions round out the loop. The darker meta-layer — heroes are real humans from doomed worlds and clearing floor 90 destroys the world — is narrative framing the engineer can treat as flavor/win-state logic rather than core combat math.

## Systems

## Premise & Meta-Structure  `[high]`
**What the game is:** A free-to-play-style mobile gacha tower-climber by in-fiction developer **Moebius (Möbius/Mobius Co.)**. The player is a **Master** who runs a base (a **Lobby** / **waiting room**), summons **Heroes**, and sends **parties of five** to clear an ascending **100-floor Tower**. Over 100 million total downloads in-fiction.

**The three defining pillars (load-bearing, 3-0 verified):**

| Pillar | Marketing name | Mechanical meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Infinite gacha | **Mobius Summon** | Every summon yields a hero who actually exists somewhere; **no duplicates ever** |
| Human-like AI | **Quanton / Quantum A.I.** | Heroes act autonomously with free will (secretly: real humans, not AI) |
| Roguelike scenario | **100-floor Tower** | Permadeath stage-by-stage climb; clearing a stage advances one floor |

**Per-account variance:** Enemies, maps, NPCs, stories, summon probabilities, missions, and difficulty all **differ per account/world**. Each Master holds the fate of exactly one world.

**Core themes:** Bond between Master and Heroes; survive and climb to floor 100 to 'save the world' / return to Earth.

**Time dilation:** 1 Earth day = 3 days in the Pick Me Up world (world runs 3x slower).

**Time-gated play loop:** Masters log in at fixed times; heroes operate autonomously while offline. A Master can be labeled 'incompetent' by others for relying solely on heroes without participating.

**Account rules:** One account per Master; rerolling impossible; account/waiting room deleted after **6 months of inactivity** (gray towers).

## Summoning & Gacha  `[high]`
**Two summon currencies / pools (3-0 verified):**

| Pool | Cost | Star range yielded |
|---|---|---|
| Free / Normal summon | **Gold** | 1★ – 3★ |
| Paid / Advanced summon | **Gems** (real money) | 3★ – 5★ |

- **Max from gacha = 5★.** 6★ requires promoting a 5★; 7★ requires the Book of Reverse Heaven (or killing a 7★).
- **No duplicates:** Mobius Summon never returns the same hero twice (each is a real existing person).
- **Per-account odds:** Summoning probabilities differ per account.
- **Tutorial pull:** Opening **10-pull** (첫 10뽑) is the tutorial draw.
- **Bonds (인연):** Heroes can be summoned linked into bond-groups (e.g. a 5-member mercenary band, a 5-member 'gale' group, a 2-member twin-mage pair: 레인 wind / 메인 cold). Pity/bond can increase hero quality.
- **Whale-bait mechanic:** A goddess can artificially inflate a hero's apparent star rank above natural worth specifically to make the summoning Master spend money / avoid frustration (e.g. Sirris shown as 4★ despite ~1-3★ true worth).
- **Class rarity:** **Mage/Wizard class is gacha-ONLY** (cannot be trained) and has a very low appearance chance.

**Cadet recruitment (a separate non-gacha acquisition channel, Niflheim example):** hero must be Lv20+, 3★+, on server 2; fee 500 gems; 10-day training; ~100:1 acceptance odds; top 3 earn permanent residence; prospects sellable to other Masters for up to 5,000 gems.

## Rarity, Promotion & Synthesis  `[high]`
**Rarity ladder: 1★ to 7★.** Profile of each tier (3-0 / 2-1 verified):

| Star | Profile | Combat | Knowledge | Arrives with |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1★ | Ordinary people (carpenters, farmers, housewives) | None | None of world/situation | Nothing; no class, no occupation |
| 2★ | Mercenaries, hunters, soldiers, informants | Minimal experience | Little more than 1★ | — |
| 3★ | Knights, talented mercenaries, wizards (some fame) | Solid | Knows they came to fight | Intermediate skills + dedicated equipment |
| 4★ | Knight commanders, high-level mages | Great | Knows world secrets (falsified) | Intermediate skills + exclusive weapon + **engraving/imprint** |
| 5★ | World-renowned heroes (may be only 1 per world) | Elite | — | Advanced skills + imprint + private weapon |
| 6★ | Promotion-only; only beatable by 7★ | Apex | — | — |
| 7★ | Anti-hero / semi-Master beings | Beyond | — | — |

**Acquisition ceilings (verified):** Gacha max = 5★ → promote to 6★ → **Book of Reverse Heaven** (역천의 서 / Book of Defiance) required for 7★ (or kill an existing 7★). 7★ created by Tell fusing 5 top users with their 6★ heroes (fatal flaw causes ranks 1-4 to fail; only rank 5/Han succeeded).

**Note:** 1★ are 'disposable' with pathetic stats and low growth. Heroes become extremely rare past 4★. Information a hero possesses scales with rank.

**Two grow paths:**

1. **Promotion (rank-up)** — done in the **Promotion Chamber** (part of Synthesis Chamber). Requires **Elemental/Attribute stones + Rank Promotion Materials** (from Daily Dungeons). Hero must reach a certain **skill level** first. Grants **new special skills** after promotion. Masters cannot see inside the chamber. Crafted-stone example: 3★ promotion needs an **Intermediate Upgrade Stone** = **Lesser Upgrade Stone + Lesser Soul Stone**. Sample costs: 2★ promotion = 10 (material/dupes), 3★ = 20.

2. **Synthesis (compounding)** — **Synthesis Chamber**, drag-drop 2+ heroes; all but one are **sacrificed** to enhance the survivor. Can transfer some abilities/traits; **>90% of a sacrifice's data is lost** (a 100-power sacrifice adds ~10, i.e. ~10%); 7★ synthesize more efficiently. Can cause loss of humanity/sanity; **bad for morale**. Can alternatively produce crafting materials (e.g. a Lesser Soul Stone). Good Masters avoid synthesis; weak heroes are 'discarded' (폐기) via it.

**Permadeath:** Exactly **two ways a hero dies permanently** — (1) killed in battle, (2) compounded/synthesized. No revival.

## Skills System  `[medium]`
**Core skill rules:**
- Heroes **auto-battle** and **learn skills on their own**.
- High-rank heroes summon already possessing skills; low-rank talents are hidden and must be sought.
- A hero can only **rank up after reaching a certain skill level**; promotion grants new special skills.
- Skills have **levels** (e.g. Lv1-6) and are tied to **floor-clear / level thresholds** (e.g. 'Throwing Defense' = Lv11 skill confirmed on floor 10; 'Siman' = Lv15 skill on floor 15; 'Incident' = Lv29 skill).
- **Skill merging:** Two skills can combine into a new one. Examples:

| Inputs | Result |
|---|---|
| Berserk + Composure | **Exceed** (Lv1) |
| Berserk + Calmness | **Ixid** (B+ grade, Unique, status-abnormal immunity; consumes vitality, can kill at max) |
| Sword Soul + Ganggyeok | **Pathology** (B grade, Unique, fixed-damage; active usable in Ixid state, self-damage to death) |
| Basic Swordsmanship + Basic Shield | **Sword-Shield Technique** (Lv3-10) → Intermediate Sword Technique |
| Max Pain Tolerance (then evolve) | **Battle Speed** (Lv1) |

- **Achievement-only skills:** e.g. **Dragon Slayer / Dragon Sal** (after killing Ban Black Dragon Halgiraf) — bonus vs dragons, cannot be trained.
- **Trained skills** (with level ranges): Basic Swordsmanship (1-5), Basic Shield (1-5), Pain Tolerance (1-5), Projectile/Throwing Defense (1-3), Insight (1-5), Flame Resistance (1-3), Stealthy Movements (Lv1, awakened via Luck), Basic Horse Riding, Power Strike, Regeneration (1-2), Berserk (1-6, +10 stat bonus, loses rationality), Composure (1-5, from synthesizing Tobi), Indomitability (slows bleed/poison).
- **Skill grades:** Letter grades exist (e.g. B, B+ for Ixid/Pathology).
- Some heroes' skills are **unreadable** due to status effects (e.g. Ridigeon's [Heavenly Sword Order]).

**Intervention points:** As a hero's rank rises, connection with Master deepens, granting **Intervention points** through which heroes can **manipulate the system**.

Tower rarity/quality varies between worlds based on inhabitant quality. **Hidden objectives** can exist beyond stated clear conditions (often tied to the world's destruction cause). ## Difficulty Walls & Progression Tiers  `[high]`
**Difficulty curve:**
- Difficulty **increases exponentially** per floor; spikes **after every 5 floors** (except event floors).
- **Floor 70** = difficulty inflection point.
- **Floor 80** = the great wall: the **Infinite Fragment Series / Shard of Infinity** (무한의 파편 시리즈) enemies appear for **ALL accounts**, exploding difficulty. Nicknamed the **'Wailing Wall'**. Described as hellish, where the living cannot survive. All Rankers who attempted it failed except the top 5.
- **Floors past 90:** Game is unfinished; **clearing floor 90 unconditionally destroys the world** (the secret win/lose condition).

**Ranker tiers (server-wide ranking, verified):**

| Floor band | Ranker tier |
|---|---|
| ~70-80 | Ranks 6th and lower |
| Floor 80 | Ranks **1st-5th only** — a tier above all others; only 5 people worldwide cleared past 80 |

**World difficulty grades (author-stated):** Worlds graded **C through S**. Difficulty multipliers: normal account = **1**, Niflheim = **~3.5**, Taonier = **~4** (S-class worldview). Townia lobby = **S-Rank** (same as Niflheimr). Per-account: enemies, maps, NPCs, stories, and missions all differ.

**Master skill in missions:** Missions include **minigames** the Master personally plays; skill improves with practice. Master can focus on or overlook specific party members during a mission.

## Currencies & Economy  `[high]`
**Currencies:**

| Currency | Type | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Gold** | Soft | Normal/free summons; in-game purchases |
| **Gems** | Premium (real money) | Advanced/paid summons; high-rank gifts; ruins exchange |

**Sample monetization package (forum):** 'One time only! Monthly package' = **150 gems + 10,000 gold per day for 30 days** = 4,500 gems + 300,000 gold total.

**Material economy:**
- **Elemental/Attribute stones + Rank Promotion Materials** — from **Daily Dungeons** (low drop rate from rare monsters).
- **Crafted stones:** Intermediate Upgrade Stone = Lesser Upgrade Stone + Lesser Soul Stone (synthesis can yield Lesser Soul Stone).
- **Ruins:** From the **Rank 8 sector** onward, 'Ruins' yield Gems and rare items exchangeable for Gems.
- **Blacksmithing facility:** Master manually crafts equipment from Tower loot.

**Sector / matchmaking economy:** Masters grouped into **sectors of 100**; sector changes **every 10 floors** (balances low vs high levels).

**Shop & gifting:** In-game shop sells morale items (glow sticks, edible treats). **Gifting** rewards heroes for clearing floors — high-rank gifts cost gems or high-value ingredients; raise favor; may unlock unexpected benefits at certain criteria. Banquets and treats boost morale.

**Gift economy caution:** Repetitive/poor gifts **lower** favorability.
